# Small-base point-center discriminator

Complete h6/h7/h8 scalar bases, dyadic inverses and valid linear fitting
completions pass. At h10 the basis still passes, but odd intersection-one
coefficients reject the linear completion. Degenerate point-star geometry at
h7 is retained explicitly. Full native frame costs and a favorable rank deficit
remain open.

The [protocol](protocol.json) records exact sources, cases, dependencies, seed
and four-worker commands. Compact complete results are in `results/`; original
logs remain in the ignored path named by the protocol.

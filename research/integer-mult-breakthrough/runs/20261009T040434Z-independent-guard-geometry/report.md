# Independent guard geometry review

Four workers checked 1,433 complete-chunk geometries at h4/5/6/8. The
independent label-set construction pairs holes in the opposite order from
the producer, restores every physical chunk, keeps transformed guards
outside child active axes, and verifies the exact positive-power majorant
and strict full-rank selected-width decrease.

The producer is read only as pinned bytes and never imported. These
geometry controls are separate from the producer complete Gaussian-field
tests and its conditional original native suppliers. No contracting
Gaussian network or new multiplication exponent is supplied.

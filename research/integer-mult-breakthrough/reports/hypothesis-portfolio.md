## Initial mathematical assessment

`kappa < a/(1+a)`. With `b=717/10^7`, `beta=1/20`, its strict ceiling is
`13623/200013623`, about `6.81104e-5`. The desired `kappa >= 1e-4`
requires `a > 1/9999` and `b > 20/189981` within this architecture,
as well as every other cost/precision/physical inequality.

This is an EXACT arithmetic obstruction under assumed inequalities, not
a lower bound on integer multiplication or on other architectures. Reducing
beta alone cannot remove it while the complex saving remains fixed.

`a=4.76829673e-5`, conditional `kappa=4.7682490470327e-5`, at
commit `0aea633524d6e54e21c077fb6af0bbc6aaa37a81`.

full Clifford support frames on branching networks, and an endpoint-aware
precision induction compatible with depth-budget recursion. The shared scatter
must also satisfy a complete new native ledger; its simplest tested incidence

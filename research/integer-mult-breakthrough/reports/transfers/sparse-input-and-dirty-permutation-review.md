## Sparse line boundary

Reviewed source: [sparse_line_input_encoding.py](../../code/obstructions/sparse_line_input_encoding.py), SHA256 `9b2eaf90f0e1f977fce5cf6553d9c0f132966ecf7f23a3bb6b71df0de5d99229`; [producer report](../obstructions/sparse-line-input-volume-boundary.md). The primary size contract is `08-assembly.tex` at `openai/math` revision `adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a`, SHA256 `763d7945b1e1ec4ebae9b799bcefcc45fa9bae6e2ffad799868a9c3d513dbd4a`. I inspected its radix-digit, padded-box and cyclic address-isomorphism statements in the pinned read-only checkout.

For any nonzero U, choose a pivot with U_p=1. In each selected column, every address belongs to one two-point orbit under translation by U. The unique member with pivot zero is the representative. Across f columns, these independent translations give exactly 2^f members per orbit. On input supported on representatives, each column contributes alpha on the representative and beta on its partner. Since `beta/alpha=-i`, the coefficient is exactly `alpha^f*(-i)^weight(p)`. No coefficient cancellation is needed to prove the orbit formula. Since `(1+i)^2=2i`, its Gaussian dyadic denominator exponent reduces to ceil(f/2). The source's inverse numerators and common unreduced denominators correctly implement A^-1 A on that restricted domain.

The original radix records have q=ceil(n/b) and T<8n/b. Therefore `q/T >= (n/b)/T >1/8`, strictly even when n/b is integral. One representative per orbit allows at most T/2^f independent original records. For f>=3 that capacity is strictly below q. An address permutation and nonzero coefficient twists do not change the count. If each record retains the original independent-coefficient semantics, a new cube needs at least 2^f q records and costs more than 2^(f-3) times T. With f=Theta(p^epsilon), epsilon fixed positive, its logarithm dominates C log p for every fixed C. This is a valid model-specific obstruction.

This argument does not bound the capacity of a differently packed payload algebra. A larger field can carry several original digit values, and a structured input map can change how information is distributed. Such alternatives require explicit packing, inverse recovery, widths, arithmetic and layouts; the record-count proof alone does not refute them. It also does not infer that every later intermediate of the original algorithm has q nonzero values.

The source's finite density samples choose independent pairs (n,radix_bits) satisfying the displayed box bounds. They do not impose the original family's additional equality b=ceil(log2 n) on those pairs. I classify those samples as controls of the algebraic density inequalities, not literal original-assembly executions. The general argument above does use the original family's actual definitions and remains valid. Likewise the epsilon=1/8 growth examples are explicitly illustrations, not a substituted multiplication exponent.

## Arbitrary fixed-dimensional dirty-bit words

Reviewed source: [borrowed_bit_permutation_words.py](../../code/obstructions/borrowed_bit_permutation_words.py), SHA256 `844ee99e9ca7c43f3cda065da14ef88a9123fc643d5110fa8667400bee276e26`; [producer report](../obstructions/borrowed-bit-permutations-and-native-shape.md).

Let a and b be the products of disjoint control sets. The completed subwords A and B satisfy `A:g->g+a` and `B:t->t+g*b` in F2, restoring every other logical bit. Starting with any dirty g0, chronological A B A B gives target increment `(g0+a)b+g0b=ab` and final g0. Recursive A may borrow the target and recursive B may borrow a control of A, because the induction restores that borrowed bit before the completed subword ends. The other subword only observes complete restored states. No intermediate value is silently presumed clean.

For k>=3, the split r=ceil(k/2) has `2<=r<k` and `2<=k-r+1<k`. Thus both recursive calls terminate. Set q=k-1. Their two smaller q values are floor(q/2) and ceil(q/2), and the two occurrences of each subword give the stated recurrence. Its recursion tree has depth at most ceil(log2 q), branching at most four, so `T(k)<=4^ceil(log2 q)<=4q^2`. This directly proves the sufficient O(k^2) bound without assuming balanced inputs have equal exact counts. The k3/4/5 counts 4/10/16 follow by substitution.

Signed controls are NOT conjugations around actual compiled gates. A Gray path with d edges uses chronological edge swaps forward followed by the first d-1 edge swaps backward. It moves its first endpoint to its last, its last to its first, and restores every intermediate vertex. For a cycle c0->c1->...->cl->c0, chronological star swaps (c0,c1),...,(c0,cl) have the required orientation. At most 2^h-1 such endpoint transpositions, each with at most 2h-1 adjacent swaps and O(h^2) gates, prove `G_h=O(2^h h^3)`. The code emits only actual gates of at most two controls; it does not count a large controlled gate as one Toffoli.

The additional dirty bit solves the parity issue by implementing P on both of its input sectors: the complete extension is P tensor I_2, whose parity is even. For h>=4, each ordinary h-bit NOT/CNOT/Toffoli is even, so an arbitrary odd data permutation cannot be inferred from those gates without an additional shape mechanism. The construction supplies a restored dirty bit rather than a initialized ancilla. At h3, the adjacent toggle already has two controls and may not need to change the borrowed bit; the source reports this distinction rather than demanding fictitious intermediate changes.

## Native shape and leverage

A Boolean word acting on h data bits and one dirty bit translates to h+1 COMPLETE address slots when applied independently to growing f columns. Those are real address coordinates inside a role stream, not free scalar banks. Every Toffoli needs three distinct live slots and a fourth distinct complete routing companion. The existing guarded [nonlinear gate](packed-nonlinear-routing.md) provides a conditional supplier with arbitrary companion restoration, only when that full shape is available. Its polynomial descriptor, complete-payload repair, all word passes and growing-h gate count remain charged.

For guarded slots of width (f+1)K with only the first f positions active, NOT and CNOT can use the same all-f Boolean-control calculation with c=1 and c=x, respectively. The original omitted-top routine on f+1 positions plus its final top operation would act on the preprocessed guard selected bit and would be the wrong endpoint. The desired specializations must explicitly restore that bit; they cannot be obtained by setting an unavailable control record to a constant one.

If the extra borrowed slot's selected bits belong to the parent's canonical C target, that slot also needs its own paid C factors. Borrowing bits at a different offset inside a guard range requires a separately proved cross-offset packed gate; same-offset compilation does not establish it. Fixed h makes G_h a constant, while growing h leaves its exponential dependence visible. Neither the restored dirty word nor the failure of an affine router supplies cheaper fixed-kernel integer products or removes the cyclic-core bootstrap obstruction.

The two results leave different useful openings: changed payload encoding could escape the sparse-record model, and paid fixed-dimensional nonlinear words can escape a prefix-affine routing restriction. Their combination still requires a complete arithmetic target, compatible stream shape, precision, stopping and a contracting full recurrence before a kappa claim.

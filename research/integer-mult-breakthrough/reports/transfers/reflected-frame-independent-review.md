## Right background and actual reflection

Let `F=C^tensorn` and `R_E=F_E*F`, an actual right-background frame. For
canonical `L_E={(a+b,b):a in Eperp,b in E}`, conjugation by `F^-1` sends
`(z,x)` to `(z,x+z)`. Thus R_E has inverse-Z Lagrangian `L_(Eperp)`,
including radical E. It follows that

```text
G_E = (F_(Eperp)*F)*F_E^-1
```

has rank zero. This means an actual monomial operator, not an identity.
For example the four-bit radical E case retains a nontrivial binary route,
XOR offset eight and global unit i. Those data are independently checked.

The common right background cancels from relatives exactly:
`(F_G*F)*(F_E*F)^-1=F_G*F_E^-1`. Moving the common background to the left
would conjugate the relative instead. These are different actual operators
and cannot be silently exchanged.

Canonical E to right F has physical map `(F_F*F)*F_E^-1`. Its one child
has Grassmann rank `d(E,Fperp)` with the reflected monomial gauge retained.
For reverse right E to canonical F, use

```text
R_E = G_(Eperp)*F_(Eperp)
F_F*R_E^-1 = (F_F*F_(Eperp)^-1)*G_(Eperp)^-1.
```

The temporal word therefore executes the inverse monomial gauge first,
then the canonical child `Eperp -> F`. Its child rank is `d(Eperp,F)`.
There is no implicit deletion of that first monomial stage or its constants.

Directly, the inverse of `R_E=F_E*F` must execute `F_E^-1` followed by
`F^-1`. The corrected actual reverse word is therefore
`F_E^-1 ; F^-1 ; F_F`. The original failed expected word executed the first
two operators in the other order. Both retained reverse fixtures independently
reject that wrong word by complete Pauli images and full matrix coefficients.
The producer's unchanged failure and recovery patch are preserved separately;
the review does not overwrite or reclassify that attempt.

## Independent literal binding

[reflected_frame_review.py](../../code/transfers/reflected_frame_review.py)
reconstructs every canonical source and target from the supplied binary
subspaces using independently checked binary bases, perpendiculars and the
literal actual-frame definitions. The reflection cases' empty
`target_subspace` field is a placeholder; their actual target is reconstructed
as Eperp and checked against the retained literal word. Forward and reverse
interface targets are reconstructed from their actual target subspace.

Each retained normal form is expanded into complete routes, quadratic phases
and selected-bit C factors. The reverse composite retains both stages in
their actual temporal order. The independent conjugation arithmetic from
the
[previous scalable-interface review](scalable-frame-independent-review.md)
compares every X and Z generator. Both words are unitary, so equality of those
images binds their quotient to a scalar. A separate exact nonzero coefficient
fixes that scalar.

Small cases are also compared at every physical matrix entry. The two
dimension-16 cases split into literal blocks of at most three bits. Their
global coefficients are computed from those independent Gaussian blocks,
without importing the producer's general quadratic Gauss-sum algorithm.
The review retains generic rank-zero local spectators `D^-2=Z` and every
global constant. For the reverse composite, the scalar includes the actual
intermediate address and monomial phase; it is not obtained by dropping
the first stage.

Corrupting the input global constant changes the actual coefficient; corrupting
the final XOR changes a Pauli image. Both mutations are rejected in every
fixture. Per-column constants repeat f times in a packed tensor, giving
their fth powers. Rank is selected width per column; physical tensor factors
are rank times f. None of those algebraic observations makes native affine
routing or buffer movement free.

The four-worker
[run](../../runs/20261009T025107Z-transfer-reflected-frame-review/report.md)
passes in 0.0595 seconds. Coverage is eight exact selected operators, not the
producer's full all-size implementation or all-subspace experiment. The
dimension-16 selected operators are bound algebraically by all Pauli images
and the separately derived scalar, without full matrix enumeration.

## Native boundary and reproduction

This supplies exact phase compatibility for a possible cross-body reuse
schedule. It does not show that a donor can die before the recipient birth,
that target clocks admit every cut response, that source history is correctly
uncomputed, or that all helper paths fit the proposed physical stock. A new
complete signed-exchange or C-primitive word must establish those obligations
and pay monomial/chirp execution on complete dirty payloads with guard buffers.

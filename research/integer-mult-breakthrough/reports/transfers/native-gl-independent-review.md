## Composition bill and full shape

Assume the three original paid operations: complete equal-width chunk exchange at `O(V L^tau)`, ordered cyclic rotation whose offset depends only on preceding fields, and a fixed two-bit address permutation in `O(V)`. Every complete payload and spectator must survive them. The packed variable-control gate uses three distinct complete `fK`-bit chunks, with selected positions `rho+jK`, `K>=6`, and arbitrary complete spectators. Write `M` for record count, `R` for payload bits, `V=MR`, `A=ceil(log2(2V))` and `delta=min(1,80(f-1)2^-K)`.

The eight rotations are bijective individually because each offset reads only the unchanged source and the other current chunk. The source-control chunk remains fixed. Reordering the target after its controls, then restoring all logical slot order, pays at most sixteen whole-chunk exchanges. Thus arbitrary slot ordering is legal. Carry-free guards prove agreement with the ideal omitted-top XOR outside the explicit exceptional set B. The ideal permutation preserves B; bijectivity and agreement outside B then prove the rotation word also preserves B. Correction `T*S^-1` is a permutation of B, not an uncharged oracle. Full destination keys, complete R-bit records, all radix passes and reinsertion account for the correction term. The top selected bit is separately paid by the elementary XOR.

The complete conditional cost of one transvection is

```text
O(V((fK)^tau+1) + M A^3 + delta M A(R+A)).
```

For a fixed invertible h-by-h binary matrix, exact row elimination expresses its route in at most `g_h<=h(h-1)+3(h-1)` self-inverse transvections. The last pivot never requires a swap; every earlier row exchange costs three additions. Reverse chronological elimination gives the forward route. Every transvection restores its arbitrary companion before the next one, so composition remains within the same complete shape and volume. The conditional GL bill is therefore

```text
O(g_h [V((fK)^tau+1) + M A^3 + delta M A(R+A)]).
```

I accept this sum under the explicit contracts. It includes the inverse route, all extracted payload work and all address/descriptor terms. It adds no Gaussian child. For fixed `h>=3`, `e=hf` differs from f by a constant. In the original fixed long-record family, `A=O(p)`, R dominates every fixed polynomial in p, `f<=d<=p`, and `K/log(p)->infinity`; dividing the two metadata/repair terms by V shows they vanish uniformly. The packed cost then has the claimed `O_h(V((eK)^tau+1))` order.

For growing h, complete payload passes cannot be absorbed into R or a constant. The displayed count gives leading movement `O(V h^(2-tau)(eK)^tau + V h^2)`, and the unsimplified descriptor/repair charges remain. Large h can be fixed once for a finite profile, but choosing it as a growing function of e changes the transfer exponent. No growing-h shortcut is accepted here.

The companion is an existing ADDRESS slot within the same role stream, with a complete equal-width range. It is not a separate scalar helper bank. There is consequently no extra Gaussian phase endpoint for the companion at the completed address gate. This conclusion is valid only for the full stream rectangle.

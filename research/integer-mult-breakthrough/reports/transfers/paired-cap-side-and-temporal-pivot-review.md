## Proper-child side capacity floor

The reviewed source is
[paired_cap_side_budget.py](../../code/complex/paired_cap_side_budget.py),
with actual center/source data from paired_five_complete_baseline.py,
paired_five_cube_discriminator.py and rigorous intervals from
characteristic.py. All complete side-role endpoints and retained kernel
coordinates are counted in its declared stock scenarios.

Put h=2p and master dimension m=3h. A serial side role in one core has
positive integer child widths whose sum is h and whose maximum is at
most h-1. For power t=1-b with 0<t<1, its minimum possible sum of powers
is attained by [h-1,1]. To see this, pad another partition with zeros:
[h-1,1,0,...] majorizes it, and concavity of x^t decreases the sum under
that concentration. Equivalently merge small parts and transfer mass
toward the largest part while retaining the h-1 cap. The three-core
per-role moment floor is therefore

    E_min = 3[(h-1)/(3h)]^t + 3[1/(3h)]^t > 1.

The strict excess follows because every normalized positive width is
less than one and x^t>x. Let A0 be the fixed unnormalized center/data
moment and W0 its complete stock. Adding R side roles permits a moment
below one only if

    A0 + R*E_min < W0+R,
    R < (W0-A0)/(E_min-1).

Using an interval upper bound for W0-A0 and lower bound for E_min-1
gives the source's conservative necessary ceiling. If A0>W0 already,
no nonnegative R works in this model. A ceiling does not construct a
side word or prove that smaller stock is attainable.

    y_t <- y_t - (d_ta/d_pa) y_p, t!=p.

post-cut shears. The net map is exactly y_t<-y_t+d_ta c_a for every
target, with y_p's entire old value cancelling from the other targets.
It holds for arbitrary old target data, arbitrary correlated helper data
and all fields. Nothing requires the pivot to be clean.

At a later channel, y_p already contains previous channel increments.

## Proposed coefficient and record interface

Reducing Z[i,1/2] by i -> 2 modulo five preserves every exact dyadic matrix identity. The completed genuine-total center decoder has no inverse of five, unlike the older pair-only decoder. A completed dyadic circuit could therefore be interpreted over F5. This does not reconstruct Gaussian coefficients: 2-i reduces to zero. Its proposed use is routing bitplanes, for which a verified 0/1 permutation endpoint returns the original Boolean data exactly.

A new native module would need the following explicit interface. A stream has M complete address records, each carrying L arbitrary F5 lanes, represented in 3L payload bits with every residue in 0 through 4. All lane values, dirty helper fields, guard coordinates and rows are arbitrary. Let R include the entire payload and retained header width, A the global address/descriptor bit length, and V=M R. Modular scalar additions and multiplications by fixed residues have constant finite-state per-lane routines and take O(V) per complete pass. A sign mask computed by a fixed-h GL transvection can be evaluated in O(A) bit work per record, if the explicit address/header or its maintained counter and return traffic are charged. Thus a sufficient metadata bill is O(V+M A), which is O(V) only under a real R>=A contract.

Long-record coefficient lanes do not by themselves provide this contract. Converting an existing bit record to 3-bit lanes causes a constant root expansion, and all copy, conversion, parking and final Boolean decode traffic is paid. Once converted, internal child records remain in the same coefficient format rather than gaining a new factor three at every level. The previous Gaussian four-field ABI is not silently identified with this modular ABI. Native placement of complete guard chunks, initial and descendant row stock, selected axes, all auxiliary lane buffers and any payload transpose remain separate interface obligations.

The natural linear-time selected-fiber scan proves no F5 Fourier supplier. The retained finite-field lookup results also do not supply a fixed log-power saving on fixed tapes. No new lookup, RAM gather or metadata oracle is assumed here.

## Genuinely smaller routing children

For f selected target bits a and immutable controls c, a GL transvection is the exact Fourier conjugation

```
X_G = Htilde_f D_G Htilde_f^-1,
D_G(a,c) = (-1)^(a dot G(c)).
```

The chronological implementation applies the inverse Fourier factor, then D_G, then the forward factor. Each Fourier factor is a complete C5_f call with paid chirps and inverse units. In a master with E=m f selected axes, each child has selected parameter ratio 1/m. These are smaller-parameter calls, not two calls at parameter E. Each still carries the entire role record and its spectators. The earlier finite router tested this exact sign-mask endpoint, complete arbitrary F5/Boolean fields and inverse; it did not price the native calls.

Assume a fully specified fixed-h circuit has W physical roles, child counts n_r for selected widths r f, and g GL transvections across every role interface and all core phases. Assume the transvection's coordinate targets are directly available in the child ABI. For a same-volume recurrence at power p=1-b, replacing the previously external GL supplier by this modular construction adds

```
Phi5(p) = (sum_r n_r (r/m)^p + 2g (1/m)^p) / W.
```

Consequently, if the previous rank deficit is delta=mW-sum_r n_r r, the new first-moment deficit is delta-2g. A necessary first-moment condition is 2g<delta. This bookkeeping does not prove the hypothetical circuit composes, has the required complete row stock or uses no extra buffer roles. A compiler must count all such roles and transvections, all literal scalar prefix traffic, output anchors and full record moves before claiming the recurrence.

If a future native module satisfies these contracts, every new wrapper child decreases E. There is no Gaussian denominator growth in its residue arithmetic. A strict complete moment and paid O(V) local overhead could then be tested in a new stopped recurrence. No such native module or full strict characteristic profile has been supplied.

## Why reducing the current word is insufficient

The independently executed [coordinate-only floor](finite-field-router-and-coordinate-bootstrap.md) already excludes separately rebuilding the weight-five source-line adapter using coordinate C children and invertible diagonals at unchanged stock: the one-rank mixed-line phase needs at least six coordinate ranks in that model. Three cores therefore add at least 15v, exceeding the nominal deficit bounded by 2v. Helpers, general address routers and joint circuit interference are outside that floor.

There are two explicit candidate implementations whose algebraic costs can also be derived before running this new source. For a direction U with five coordinates, Fourier conjugation of the diagonal i^(parity_U) uses two C5_(5f) calls, total selected rank 10f rather than the line's f. An explicit coordinate GL maps e0 to U with four CNOTs and returns with four inverse CNOTs. Each CNOT costs two C5_f calls, in addition to the central C5_f child, for total rank 17f. Their proposed literal replays in the new source are unexecuted.

For p12 paired coordinates, h=24, v=25344, q=265 independently dirty pair/total roots, the copied-center deficit is 31608. Holding its stock and master fixed, the coordinate-only lower bound gives deficit at most -348552. The explicit two-Fourier method adds 27v and gives -652680. The explicit eight-transvection line wrapper adds 48v and gives -1184904. These numerical substitutions are analytical arithmetic; they are not outputs from the unrun source. The first-moment failures are scoped to separately replaced source-helper line adapters. They are not a universal lower bound on modular circuits, multi-bank constructions, global wrapper cancellation or a changed master.

The important distinction is therefore precise: the new F5 type can make wrapper children smaller and remove Gaussian precision, but the known finite dyadic word does not thereby gain a contracting self-contained routing profile. A new word, shared/batched wrapper mechanism, independently supplied modular native primitive, or different address family must change the paid ledger. Simple direct-sum amplification or a renamed ordinary bit atom is not evidence for such a change.

## Independent geometric explanation

For any binary subspace U of dimension r in dimension h, the displayed L_U
is Lagrangian, including degenerate U. The symplectic pairing between its
two summands vanishes because `U^perp` is orthogonal to U. Its dimension is h.
Its distances from I and Full are r and h-r respectively.

If v is odd and belongs to U, the intersection of L_U with the line-frame
graph `x=v(v dot z)` has dimension `h-r+1`, so their distance is r-1.
Indeed its vectors have `z=n+epsilon*v`, `x=epsilon*v`, with n in U-perp;
the odd norm of v makes the graph equation hold. If s is odd and belongs to
U-perp, the analogous kernel graph `x=z+s(s dot z)` intersects L_U in
dimension r+1, giving distance h-r-1. Its vectors have `z=u+epsilon*s`,
`x=u`, with u in U.

For arbitrary U and V, the same calculation gives

```text
d(L_U,L_V) = dim(U)+dim(V)-2*dim(U intersect V).
```

Consequently nested source spaces give geodesic frame paths without the
nondegenerate subspace restriction. A scalar DAG node with source space U
orthogonal to its downstream target space M may therefore use this chart
despite a radical witness that blocked the earlier nested projector chart.
This corrects the scope of that obstruction. The individual trimmed subset
features have odd labels in both their source and target supports, so this
orthogonality premise fails there; their complete cancellation chronology
still needs a different integration argument.

These statements concern labels and transition ranks. Literal realizations,
all normalization units and affine translations remain essential. In
particular the two complementary literal common frames do **not** multiply

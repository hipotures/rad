# Direct activity self-bootstrap and the packet-width boundary

Status: **EXACT CONSEQUENCES OF DECLARED SUFFICIENT COST LEDGERS**.
The unchanged direct activity construction cannot fund its own ordinary
routing through the unchanged balanced assembly. Dividing the selected axes
into smaller tensor packets does not reverse the power comparison. These
are certification boundaries for the stated upper bounds, not time lower
bounds on every router, zeta transform or integer multiplier.

## Three different savings

Use alpha for an independently available ordinary routing saving, so its
exchange exponent is tau=1-alpha. Use b for the desired activity/complex
characteristic saving, so its proposed potential power is p=1-b. Use a for
the parameter returned by the balanced integer assembly. The retained
assembly requires

    a < (1-beta)b,       kappa < a/(1+a),       0 < beta < 1.

These quantities are not interchangeable. In particular, an available
ordinary bit atom may have alpha>a. Whether it is independently supplied,
or instead inferred from the new proposed assembly, is a substantive input.
The following conflict includes alpha=a and the weaker case alpha<=a.

The actual inherited routing interface is reviewed in
[the ordinary exponent note](activity-native-routing-exponent.md) and
[the public paid balanced-transfer review](pr163-paid-balanced-transfer-independent-review.md).
The improved public ordinary parameter used as one conditional comparison
here is reconstructed from pinned PR163 head

    theta = 594323213359226694617/10^24,
    a_old = 384599/10^10,
    alpha = (1-theta)a0 + theta a_old.

supplier and assembly assumptions remain conditional. This experiment

## Sufficient direct-ledger conflict

For root selected width d, K=d^c and cutoff e>=d^beta, the local movement
term V(eK)^tau log(e+1) is absorbed by the proposed activity power if

    tau(1+c/beta) < p < 1.

The logarithm is paid by the strict power gap. This is the declared sufficient
interface; a cheaper actual route could support a different inequality.
Let m be its exact margin:

    m = 1-b-(1-alpha)(1+c/beta).

If alpha<=a<(1-beta)b, then, even setting c=0,

    m <= alpha-b <= a-b < -beta b < 0.

Consequently no positive choice of c repairs this interface. Shrinking beta
does not help while the same strict balanced assembly relation and positive
saving remain. A polylogarithmic K removes its positive power contribution,
but still leaves the conflicting requirement alpha>b, as well as its own
exceptional-density and metadata obligations.

The alternate H stopping potential requires p>=tau and H<d, where
H=K^[tau/(1-tau)]. Here p>=tau means alpha>=b, already contradicted by
alpha<=a<(1-beta)b. Replacing one cutoff by the other therefore does not
make this self-dependent certification valid.

An independently stronger ordinary supplier is outside that contradiction.
The exact control distinguishes the conditional public-alpha small-b regime,
which fits the direct power comparison, from b=1/1000 with the same atom,
which does not. A hypothetical still stronger atom is explicitly hypothetical.
Using a routing-compatible alpha as the new assembly output a can violate
the balanced inequality itself; the separate conflation negative checks this.

With a fixed available alpha and this direct ledger, b<alpha and a<b imply
kappa<alpha/(1+alpha) within the retained assembly. This is a ceiling on
claims certified by this combination of supplied bills. It is not a general
multiplier ceiling, and does not constrain the public construction's different
finite circuit/master normalization. A new activity word alone cannot claim
a large scale improvement by silently using the atom it is intended to prove.

## Tensor packetization and complete volume

Let E be the complete selected-axis count and choose q=Theta(E^gamma),
0<gamma<=1. Partition those axes into Theta(E/q) packets of at most q axes,
with a paid small final packet if needed. Exact tensor factorization is

    Z_E = product over packets [Z_packet tensor I_other_axes].

Every packet operator acts on every complete row and every input record.
They are serial tensor factors, not disjoint data blocks. If V=M R is the
actual four-field payload volume, each packet retains that V. Giving it only
qV/E because its selected width is q would omit entire input fibers.

For an attempted proof still paying the inherited router separately, let
s=(1-beta)b be the prospective stopped packet saving. Its proposed recursive
contribution V q^(1-s) and remaining routing V(qK)^tau give, after all packets,

    O(V E q^-s + V E q^-alpha K^(1-alpha)),

apart from separately charged logarithms and metadata. With global
K=E^c, the two powers of E are

    desired:  1-gamma s,
    routing:  1-gamma alpha + c(1-alpha).

The strict absorption comparison is therefore

    gamma(alpha-s) > c(1-alpha).

If alpha<=a<s, it fails even for c=0 and every positive gamma. A smaller
packet reduces the prospective saving and the ordinary routing saving by
the same factor; retaining a global K can only make the comparison worse.
The independently supplied complete packet transform would already include
its routing, so one must not recharge this term if that stronger full theorem
exists. The displayed two-term comparison concerns an unfinished proof using
the same inherited local bill, rather than a lower bound on a genuinely new
packet supplier.

For constant q the serial factorization yields no fixed power saving in E.
For polylogarithmic q it gives at most an iterated-log improvement under this
particular estimate. Redefining K separately for each packet changes the
physical address slot, spectator planes and exceptional-density construction;
that conversion is a new paid interface, not a numeric substitution.

The full all-width recursive router from
[the preceding package](irregular-activity-routing-and-transfer.md) can be
used inside a sufficiently wide q packet. Its twelve complete selected guard
ranges come from that packet's existing axes and are processed exactly once;
their K-1 planes remain spectators. The toy controls here use q=2 or 3 only
to check tensor algebra and volume accounting. They do not instantiate that
twelve-guard native layout.

## Bills that a changed packet route must actually alter

The full target slot is qK address bits, while other selected axes become row
parameters. The complete long record width R and all Gaussian fields remain
unchanged. Mask decoding and permutation generation still read the complete
actual header; unless a new representation is proved, replacing A by q
discards other row labels needed for placement and restoration.

There are Theta(E/q) forward/undo schedules, each with its own current-mask
setup. Their complete exceptions have total density conservatively
O(E 2^-K), not just the root packet's O(q 2^-K). Uniform setup and repair
must be checked against the actual full R; short Python integers or random
mask evaluations cannot certify those traffic costs. If a new route truly
avoids full-slot exchanges, its target/control ordering, reciprocal updates,
every complete companion, padding and reverse must be replaced explicitly.

Completed nonunit Z packets also accumulate magnitude. For all-one input,
the highest output coefficient of Z_E is 2^E, even if each packet has q axes.
A guard based only on 2^q is insufficient. In a complete fixed-grid or
rounding argument, completed packet endpoints add their selected-width charges
over the whole chain, giving O(E), while child subcalls within one packet
may still decrease geometrically. The outer target tolerance, denominator
grid and full dirty/buffer state remain part of that proof. This observation
is compatible with the earlier geometric endpoint lemma; it does not supply
the absent faster packet word or outer integer recovery.

A simultaneous or fused implementation could escape serial full-volume
traffic. It must compute the tensor product, rather than applying different
axis factors to different old rows, and preserve a literal complete-field
allocator and endpoint. Packed product embeddings, controlled-line frames,
or a different master are genuine new interfaces; their missing costs cannot
be removed by labeling an unfinished same-volume proof as a packet supplier.

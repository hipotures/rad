The conditional proof includes every copied, parked and dirty coordinate

an interval bound Gamma squared, and final error at most
`N*Gamma*2^-q` when every scalar write error is counted. The intermediate
magnitude proof uses the interval bound as well as the input prefix bound.
These steps do not rely on random error cancellation or polynomially many
recursive nodes. Fixed branching and linear depth bound the logarithm of
the injection count, which is sufficient for an additive precision charge.

The exact dyadic reference's trailing-zero reductions are mathematical
instrumentation. They supply neither a physical grid conversion nor an
all-size exact denominator theorem. The rounded word instead retains one
common grid and includes transient numerator allowances. Completed zero
padding can be projected with its residue charged; intermediate correlated
scratch cannot be declared zero. Both bounded families reject premature
projection through corruption of the retained bank, rather than only the
discarded coordinates.

I accept this numerical lemma under its stated complete-state and uniform
norm assumptions. Zeta endpoints with exponentially growing norm, hidden
width-dependent scalar gauges and a free address compiler are outside that
contract. A geometrically decreasing-width extension is a separate live
hypothesis. The [product recovery review](../transfers/rounded-product-independent-review.md)
supplies a compatible sufficient coefficient-recovery interface, but the
actual native product costs, layouts and complete integer assembly still

# Independent review of general flags and ordered scans

This coordinator review accepts the stated field algebra and the all-size
scan obstructions. It does not certify a native supplier, a global dirty-bank
circuit or a multiplier exponent. The synthesis track independently authored
the finite constructors and controls; this review rederives their proof
arguments rather than interpreting successful tests as proofs.

For image flags V contained in U and kernel flags K0 contained in K1, an
idempotent exists exactly when V meets K0 trivially and U+K1 is the whole
space. Let A annihilate K1 and have full row rank a. Every admissible image F
surjects under A. Its intersection with ker(A) contains V intersect ker(A),
so dim(F) is at least dim(V)+a-rank(A restricted to V). Adding independent
A responses from U attains this minimum and preserves disjointness from K0.
A complement to U intersect K0 attains the maximum. Nested complements inside
K1 give commuting minimum/maximum endpoint projectors. This independently
confirms the [general flag theorem](../synthesis/general-idempotent-image-kernel-flags.md).

With K1=V-perp and K0=U-perp under the same nonsingular symmetric G, the
minimum reduces to dim(V)+radical-dimension(V), and the maximum to
dim(U)-radical-dimension(U). Allowing nonselfadjoint projectors alone therefore
does not remove the static paired-source obstruction. A useful changed
chronology must alter actual image or covector flags, with every residual
and scalar support constraint retained. Good-prime-power matrix controls
require unit denominators and cleared bases; they are not blanket ring claims.

An ordered inclusive prefix matrix has an inverse with at most two nonzero
entries in each row. Permutations and invertible diagonal gauges preserve
that bound. A product of s such inverse matrices has at most 2^s nonzero
entries per row. The inverse Boolean zeta matrix on f coordinates has a row
with all 2^f entries nonzero. Consequently a one-bank product consisting only
of invertible prefix scans, with arbitrary orders and nonzero gauges, needs
s at least f. This argument was supplied independently by the coordinator
before the synthesis track's mixed-product investigation.

For two scans, the synthesis track supplied a stronger mixed-type argument.
A column of prefix-times-diagonal-times-difference is a weighted difference
of two nested suffix indicators in the prefix order. Its support is one
interval if the common tail cancels, or one suffix if it remains. The dual
statement holds for rows of difference-times-diagonal-times-prefix. External
nonzero diagonal gauges preserve these supports. The f coordinate-filter
supports of Boolean zeta distinguish every Boolean membership pattern, so
any ordering of its 2^f rows needs at least 2^f-1 adjacent membership changes.
If every filter is an interval there are at most 2f changes. For f at least
three this is impossible. Arbitrary boundary permutations only relabel the
ordering and patterns. Difference-times-difference has at most four nonzero
entries per row and also fails for f at least three. Together these arguments
exclude all four two-scan type pairs in that precise invertible one-bank model.

The synthesis package separately enumerates all 2304 fixed-label f2 order/type
cases and binds two surviving prefix-prefix zero-pattern candidates to exact
minor contradictions. That finite result is stronger at f2 but does not
include arbitrary boundary permutations. Its interval controls and modular
reductions are bounded controls; the preceding all-size claims are analytical
proofs. Dirty borrowed-bank shears, three or more mixed scans, projections,
other gate sets and changed precision interfaces remain outside the exclusion.

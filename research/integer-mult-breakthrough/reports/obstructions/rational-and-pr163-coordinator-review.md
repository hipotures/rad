The rational [completion proof](../synthesis/rational-frame-completion-and-paid-chains.md)
is correct. For `V` inside `U`, the obstruction is
`V intersect rad(U)`, equivalently `rad(V) intersect rad(U)`. If it vanishes,
the pairing of `rad(V)` with `U` has full row rank, so rational dual partners
exist. Removing their projections onto a nondegenerate complement in `V`
leaves a hyperbolic block with Gram determinant `(-1)^r`. This attains
`dim(V)+r`; injectivity into the radical quotient proves the maximum.
The argument applies to arbitrary rational symmetric forms. The stronger
`r<=1` simplification uses the real signature of the specific `I-J/9`
form when `h>9`, and is not automatically valid over finite fields.

single-gate changes globally optimal: neighboring frames change each
other's lower spans and future caps. The first 32-cut negative discriminator
supports a connected-block search rather than repeated identical local
optimization. Actual basis height, prime exclusions, representation and

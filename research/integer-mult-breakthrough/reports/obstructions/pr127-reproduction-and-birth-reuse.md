## Why birth reuse is a structural direction

A clean scalar program can reset a newly born role to zero. A reversible dirty
implementation instead retains its actual old value g and immediately
subtracts D*g from the outputs, where D is that birth's future output response.
Backward response propagation must stop at later births of the same role.
The uncancelled dirty contribution is exactly D*g even when g depends on
earlier sources or reused roles. The immediate subtraction cancels it. Reversing
the actual source injections and shears in their true chronological order then
restores the original dirty workspace. Grouping all negative source injections
at the end is generally incorrect after role aliasing.

The physical implementation still needs a legal common frame at each birth,
complete readout responses, exact scalar growth bounds and restoration of all
declared endpoints. PR127 obtains suitable recipients through deferred reads.
Its dead donor frame E is contained in the recipient birth frame sigma. It
excludes a proper residual with equal characteristic vectors because the
required alternating normal form is not supplied by its inherited contract.

There is also a useful general paid-moment deduction. Suppose m>h and
`0<=e<=s<h`. A legal merge removes children `h-e` and `m-h+s`, adds `s-e`,
and removes one unit of physical stock in the same normalization. Their
weighted rank difference is exactly m. Put p=1-b, with `0<b<1`. The two
removed widths sum to `m+(s-e)` and both lie strictly between s-e and m.
Strict concavity of x^p therefore gives

```text
(h-e)^p + (m-h+s)^p - (s-e)^p > m^p.
```

If the old normalized numerator is `A/(W*m^p)<=1`, the new one is
`(A-delta)/((W-1)*m^p)`, where delta is the left side above. For W>1 it is
strictly smaller than the old moment. This is a conditional potential lemma
for a legal merge; it supplies no missing circuit, native supplier or exponent.
Actual multiplicities and stock must use the same paid units. In the public
two-axis witness one merge is replicated 2v times, so both changes are scaled
by that factor. Free reset, unpaid readout or incompatible frames would
invalidate the deduction's application.

## Research consequence and limits

The external result justifies a fresh portfolio direction: schedule-aware
reuse of arbitrary dirty roles with cut responses. It does not justify an
automatic merge or a public leaderboard sweep. In the campaign's current
per-edge geodesic side, most retired helpers have higher-dimensional frames
than fresh source-line births, so simple containment matching cannot reuse
them. Larger recipient birth frames, source grouping, shared side features
or a joint cancellation topology are distinct hypotheses worth testing.

The retained copied-stream, residual-to-child, translated-gauge, complete-row,
exact-recovery, prime, all-size and fixed-tape hypotheses remain assumptions
of the external package. An [independent compact transfer review](../transfers/pr127-transfer-independent-review.md) reconstructs its three moments, aggregate child rebilling, scalar bill and seven final margins. It does not independently repeat all 47 constraints or prove the inherited native suppliers. The finite reproduction is accepted; a complete
new campaign multiplication theorem remains open.

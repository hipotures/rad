Every root follows the same growing binary source span E. The helpers and roots
at a center addition use the identical actual representative `F_E`, including
its phase, affine routing and global unit.

Write M for the pair-incidence map and D for the central decoder. Initially
the helper and root virtual values are arbitrary `z_g` and `z_r`. The old-read
phase computes M at identity, subtracts `D(z_r+Mz_g)` from the targets, and
undoes M. Thus the original root seed is retained in addition to the helper
ancestor response. Inject every source x into its helper at its actual odd
line frame. Process the sources in the pinned order: grow every root to E,
move the current helper to that same E, write it to its ten incident roots,
and retire it at full. All roots finish at full since the paired source labels
span the entire h-dimensional space.

Each late root therefore contains the physical stream
`F_full(z_r+M(z_g+x))`. Copy its **complete** role stream into one owned scratch
stream, apply `F_full^-1` only to the copy, and scatter D while the targets
remain at common identity. Dispose of that scratch before processing the next
root. The two reads cancel the complete dirty response and retain `DMx`.
At full, undo M to restore every original root to `F_full*z_r`, then uninject
the source to restore each helper to `F_full*z_g`. Mutable original sources
must already have been restored before this uninject.

splice all source injections must occur **before K**, and the literal full-frame

## Scalar mechanism and same-singleton fusion

The labels choose one coordinate from each of three distinct coordinate pairs.
Thus, for eleven pairs, `v = 8 binom(11,3)`. Let

    B[T,S] = (|T intersection S| - 1)/2.

Restrict `B` to each eight-port cube with the same three coordinate pairs,
calling that block matrix `B_cube`. The complete scalar identity is

    H = B_cube - B,       K = I - B_cube,       H + B + K = I.

The audit reconstructs every H coefficient from signed, disjoint source-support
unions. It checks that the copied centers are the complete singleton stars and
that their scatters are exactly `1/3` on containing targets and `-1/6` elsewhere.
It also checks `K^2=I` on every cube and the rank-three, opposite-parity source
frames required by K. It uses injective balanced integer digits with base
8,192 and a conservative absolute coefficient bound 3,161; this checks all
source columns simultaneously and is not modular sampling.

There are exactly 1,320 roots broadcasting to a single target. Their signed
channels have already been combined before the readout. The construction
retains conservative source supports even when different final contributions
cancel: it does not infer a smaller physical value span from a canceled scalar
coefficient. This is the relevant distinction from simply shortening an XOR
or scalar addition circuit.

The copied coordinate-star frame has dimension 20 and radical dimension one
for every coordinate. Its full center payment is

    loss = 22 * 20 = 440.

These are degenerate binary subspaces. The interpretation uses the full
Lagrangian family `L_U`, rather than an orthogonal projector onto a purportedly
nondegenerate U. In this family zero and full U have the required identity and
full-transform anchors, and a nested U-to-V transition has charged width
`dim(V)-dim(U)`, subject to its actual phase/monomial reconciliation contract.
Binary labels alone do not make actual frame representatives equal.

## Why the paid entry survives reuse

Every recipient is untouched before its compensation read. The donor is dead
before that read, and its last frame is contained in the recipient entrance
gauge. A physical chain therefore has the literal sequence

    zero -> donor frames -> donor final U -> recipient gauge
         -> recipient frames -> full.

The U-to-gauge transition is included. The construction removes the donor's
old independent full-frame tail and the recipient's separate physical slot;
it does not make the entrance transition free. Exact chronology is important:
the independent reconstruction sorts the center phase first, then the remaining
operations, and orders deferred target reads by their actual deadlines.

All old independent gauges have dimension 18, with exterior children of width
54 after the three-block sharing. The 2,640 splices remove rank
`2,640 * 66 = 174,240` and the same amount of ambient role capacity.
Before reuse `W=18,321` and rank is `1,207,866`; after reuse `W=15,681`
and rank is `1,033,626`. Both have deficit 1,320. The unchanged deficit is
therefore measured against less persistent stock. Descending operation frames
also change the complete width distribution while preserving its first moment;
that effect must be evaluated from the full histogram, rather than a mean-only
argument for a concave moment.

The raw dirty scalar justification is separate from this geometric recount.
If a recipient's virtual seed is the donor's current value z at birth, its
full downstream decoder column `D_recipient` contributes
`D_recipient z`. Subtracting this exact contribution immediately before the
untouched recipient's first use cancels the inherited seed for arbitrary z.
All actual physical scalar gates must then be reversed in chronological order,
with their signed inverse coefficients; source mutation K must be undone
before source cleanup. This gives a general compensation argument only when
the decoder column, lifetime assumptions and literal inverse word are correct.
The upstream complete formal all-column check supplies that separate finite
binding. Our verifier does not claim to reproduce it.

## Why three cores can share one stock here

For a completed core with entrance frame T_sigma, arbitrary raw dirty input z
has virtual meaning `z_hat=T_sigma^-1 z`. A dirty-transparent complete core
therefore has raw operator

    U = F_A T_sigma^-1,       U^-1 = T_sigma F_A^-1.

Its inverse must include the complete compensation, center reads, scalar
cleanup and actual endpoint gauges. Inverting only the positive producer DAG
does not meet this interface.

The construction places the three completed local invocations in three
orthogonal h-dimensional address blocks of an ambient `m=3h` space. Each
physical auxiliary slot participates sequentially, and the stage routing is a
bijection of actual role slots. The three residual actions consequently tensor
on distinct address blocks. Their exterior correction is one child of width
`3 dim(sigma)`, including its actual phases and the final rank-zero adapter.
After all physical births have been spliced, no positive-width exterior gauge
remains in the selected profile.

This mechanism differs from our failed same-h three-body helper word. That
word paid three independent h-dimensional traversals against one h-dimensional
role stock. Here three traversals match one 3h-dimensional stock. It also lies
outside our original four-port serial support bound: it uses many source ports,
an explicit invertible source-bank mixer K, a shared scalar word and coordinated
births. Our earlier nested nondegenerate-frame obstruction does not extend to
these degenerate L_U frames. None of those scoped negative results is a general
obstruction to this construction.

The complete scalar and phase routing cost remains significant. The finite
group factor `|O(66,2)|` cancels from the normalized moment but remains in role
stock, row counts, routers and guard constants. K, the copied centers, all
compensation reads, inverse cleanup, both width-two data finishes and all
three local invocations remain charged. This review does not establish an
integer multiplier or a new kappa from the finite profile alone.

# Actual centers permit a first-threshold side test, but cap the unchanged larger target

At nine paired coordinates, the complete actual center profile leaves room
for a shared side architecture at complex saving `b=1e-4`. It cannot reach
the coordinator's reported comparator or `b=1e-3`, even with zero side
stock. At twelve pairs, the strongest ideal side floor barely permits the
separately retained cap stock at the comparator, but actual bucket/cap/full
pieces already exclude that named model. A larger-scale attempt therefore
needs a changed center or shared-stock chronology, rather than a long sweep
of the same center/side architecture.

These are exact moment sensitivity results for explicitly stated profiles.
No permitted optimistic profile is an attained native supplier. No upstream
exponent is adopted. Sources are
[`paired_cap_side_budget.py`](../../code/complex/paired_cap_side_budget.py)
and the separate immutable extension
[`paired_cap_leader_budget.py`](../../code/complex/paired_cap_leader_budget.py).
They read the frozen complete baseline's actual center tree and source word,
and the existing exact outward moment arithmetic. The extension does not
alter the original source or its completed run.

Let `h=2p`, `v=32*binom(p,5)`, `m=3h`, and `q=2p(p-1)`. The actual
copied-center implementation retains `10v` source-copy roles and incurs
loss `q(h-4)`. Its complete prefix/retirement/copy histogram is regenerated
exactly, not replaced by its first moment. Original source children are
`[4,h-6,1]` per source per core; the two retained width-two calls per
original source are also counted. With no side roles, stock is `W0=12v`,
and the complete first-moment deficit is `2v-3q(h-4)`.

Two target-clock models are kept separate. The first gives each target one
optimistic child `[h-1]`. The second uses `[5,h-6]`, reflecting a local
common rank-five cut before the final target kernel. Splitting a child raises
its subunit moment. Neither model asserts that every cross-cap target
deadline can actually be satisfied.

For a retained side role whose serial geodesic path has total rank `h` in
each core and whose positive integer child widths are at most `h-1`,
concavity minimizes its moment at `[h-1,1]`. The three-core floor is

    c_side(b)=3*((h-1)/m)^(1-b)+3*(1/m)^(1-b) > 1.

If the fixed center/data moment is `C0(b)` before division by stock, the
necessary inequality is

    R_side*(c_side(b)-1) < W0-C0(b).

Thus a negative fixed margin excludes every nonnegative side stock within
this serial completed-role class. A positive margin yields only a necessary
upper ceiling. The bound does not apply to changed endpoints, removed roles,
different coupled recurrences, arbitrary block-bank operations or a side
helper without this serial path contract. Native wrapper cost is omitted
optimistically, not declared free.

The original full run tests seven pairs as a failure control, nine pairs at
the first threshold, and twelve as a larger-scale comparison. At `p9`, the
side ceilings are:

| Component saving b | One target child: necessary R_side/v ceiling | Target through U5: necessary ceiling |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 0.0001 | 53.9293 | 53.4936 |
| 20/189981, about 0.000105274 | 50.3970 | 49.9613 |
| 0.001 | No nonnegative stock fits | No nonnegative stock fits |

The `20/189981` boundary is the inherited `beta=1/20` assembly's necessary
complex threshold for `kappa=1e-4`; it is not a certified achievable saving.
Binary and outer transfer obligations still apply. At `p7`, the fixed deficit
is `-1176`, so every tested positive saving fails. At `p12`, the actual
center/data profile also fails at `b=0.001` even without side roles: the
optimistic one-target-child moment is greater than `1.0001097437`.

The side stock comparisons are architectural hypotheses:

| p | Distinct raw cap aggregate and kernel stock per source | Readable/parked directions per source cube | Independent source-copy leaf stock per source |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 7 | 5 | 90 / 70 | 15 |
| 9 | 210/32 = 6.5625 | 140 / 70 | 30 |
| 12 | 211/32 = 6.59375 | 141 / 70 | 31 |

All raw and parked coordinates are retained. This is not a universal role
floor: shared kernel storage, existing original banks or temporal births may
change it. Conversely, the raw aggregate count does not supply its source
formation. An independent copy tree can require the larger leaf stock. At
`p9` the disjoint source/target intersection is unavailable, so the `j0`
channel is correctly absent.

The additional raw-cap model assigns each retained coordinate its input
bucket, common-cap entrance and full endpoint. For `j>=1` these pieces are
`[6-j,j-1,h-5]`, omitting a zero piece; `j0` uses `[6,h-6]`. All raw
coordinates, including kernel parking, make a complete ambient traversal.
Their formation from original source lines, shared birth stock and global
readout are explicitly unverified. For `p9`, this raw-cap model with target
`[5,h-6]` has moment approximately `0.9996872451` at `b=0.0001` and
`0.9996970585` at `20/189981`. This justifies a small structural sharing
test at the first threshold, not a parameter sweep or exponent claim.

The coordinator separately requested comparison against the reported
decimal `kappa=0.0006558894`. Under the inherited assembly inequalities
`a<(1-beta)b` and `kappa<a/(1+a)`, the necessary boundary even as beta
tends to zero is

    b > kappa/(1-kappa) = 0.0006563198732478726... .

At `beta=1/20` the stronger lower boundary is approximately
`0.0006908630244714449`. A changed assembly must derive its own equations.
These decimal values are comparison inputs supplied by the coordinator,
not independent verification or adoption of the reported multiplier.

The p12 necessary side ceiling with a U5 target cut is about `6.43481v`,
below the independent raw stock `6.59375v`. At fixed `beta=1/20`, even the
single-target-child ideal raw-stock floor is above one. At `b=0.0007` the
exclusions strengthen. These failures concern the stated fixed actual
center profile and separately retained cap architecture; they do not prove
that all paired-five implementations fail.

All classifications above are separated from one by exact rational outward
intervals. Logarithms use a bounded atanh series after power-of-two
reduction; exponentials use a positive series with an explicit geometric
tail. The retained endpoints are rounded only outwards on a power-two grid.
Human-readable decimals are illustrations of those exact bounds. The
complete side role endpoints and fixed profile rank telescope are checked;
omitting an endpoint is rejected. Scalar gates, full-stream precision,
record movement, inactive payloads and a well-founded supplier remain
additional obligations. Temporary native address work is not silently
converted into persistent scalar-bank credit.

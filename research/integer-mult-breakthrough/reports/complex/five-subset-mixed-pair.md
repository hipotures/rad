**HYPOTHESIS.** A new odd-weight label family might change the attainable
scale. The following exact scalar and binary-frame lemmas are useful
components. They are not a complex network or a conditional exponent.
The coordinator and complex agent independently identified the degree-two

All work was assisted by OpenAI Codex.

The read-only [prior five-subset screen](../../../integer-multiplication-bounds/reports/five-subset-motif-screen.md)

itself is not a novelty claim or an escape from that earlier result's premises.

## Exact scalar identity

Inputs and targets are five-subsets of [h]. Write t=|S intersect T| and

    p(t)=(t-1)(t-3)/8=(2*binom(t,2)-3t+3)/8.

Then p(5)=1, p(1)=p(3)=0. For distinct S,T, nonzero coefficients occur
only at even intersections 0,2,4, where five-subset binary labels are
orthogonal. The exact side correction is -p(t) at those even intersections
and zero at odd intersections. Central plus side is the identity matrix.
All coefficients are Gaussian dyadic.

Put T0=sum x_U, G_i=sum(x_U:i in U), and P_ab=sum(x_U:{a,b} subset U).
The central output is

    (1/4)*sum(P_ab:{a,b} subset S)
      -(3/8)*sum(G_i:i in S)+(3/8)*T0.

Each contained point belongs to exactly four source pairs, so
`G_i=(1/4)*sum(P_ij:j!=i)`. Singleton retained centers are unnecessary.
Recovering the total from sum(P)/10 would introduce a factor 1/5.

Instead replace exactly two selected pair centers by D_ab=T0-P_ab,
and retain P_ab for all other pairs. Every source contributes to ten
pair stars. Therefore

    T0=(sum(unreplaced P)-sum(replaced D))/8,
    replaced P=T0-D.

The entire scatter is dyadic and uses exactly binom(h,2) retained features.
The total may be an internal scalar node; it is not a designated center.
This removes both the h singleton centers and the extra retained total.

Independent track C identified a precision charge missed by a bare
dyadicity statement. After eliminating G, set
`alpha_J=(1/4)[J subset S]-(3/32)|J intersect S|` and
`gamma=3/8+sum(alpha_J:J replaced)`. The substituted scatter coefficient
is `alpha_J+gamma/8` on an unreplaced P_J and its negative on a D_J.
With replaced pairs {0,1},{0,2}, target {1,4,5,6,7}, and unreplaced
pair {0,3}, gamma=9/32 and the scatter coefficient is 9/256.
Therefore the primitive needs eight denominator bits. The legacy
seven-bit allowance cannot be inherited. A future literal scalar
gate-count/depth envelope must pay this charge before any transfer claim.

The transparent scalar dirty wrapper subtracts the old scatter, gathers
the fresh source contribution, adds the new scatter, and undoes the gather.
For arbitrary old centers z, the two scatters total R(z+Gx)-Rz=RGx,
and every center returns to z. Side plus central is the identity shear.
This is scalar algebra only; physical phase assignment and carrier counts
for a complete side circuit are still open.

## Exact pair-feature binary frame

For J={a,b}, the span W_J of binary five-subset indicators containing J
has dimension h-2. Its orthogonal complement consists of vectors constant
outside J whose two inside entries sum to that outside constant. A basis is

    u=e_a+e_b,
    v=sum(e_i:i outside J)+e_a.

Its Gram matrix is `[[0,1],[1,(h-1) mod2]]`, with determinant one.
Thus W_J is nondegenerate for either parity of h, despite u being isotropic.
This is stronger than an unsupported coordinate-frame enlargement.
The general nondegenerate-residual Gauss normal form still needs to be
included when an alternating residual appears; orthonormal bases are
not being assumed automatically.

A total and a replaced D_J each have full support span h for h>=8.
For D_J, its support contains all five-subsets avoiding a and all avoiding
b, and those two coordinate hyperplanes span the full space. Its scalar
construction T0-P_J may take place in the full frame, including the paid
increase from W_J. No high-to-low decrease is credited for this gather.

Under the inherited retained-center return rule, local center loss is

    L0=binom(h,2)*(h-2)+4.

Without this mixed-pair reduction, explicit pair/singleton/total centers
cost `binom(h,2)*(h-2)+h*(h-1)+h`.

### Literal paid copied-center frame sequence

The scalar substitution does not scatter from the small pair-star frame.
It uses icekylinx's copied-center lemma, pinned at
`11817ccacb564bb7f98789c20dc11d3fece207e3`, file
`notes/copied-centers-lemma.tex`, with the retained Paureel complete-stream
copy/transform/read/discard interface. Keep its source notices and scope.
Let `D0=B tensor F`, `D1=(B perpendicular P) tensor F`, and
`DU=D0 perpendicular (P tensor U)` for a retained feature frame U of rank r.

In the forward invocation, the middle mixer leaves the original center
at DU. Copy its complete physical stream, transform only the copy from
DU to D0 with rank r, use this copy for every unchanged scatter target
at common frame D0, and erase the temporary. The original is unchanged
by those reads. Advance it directly DU->D1 with rank h-r before cleanup.
Its original endpoint and every later scalar incidence are unchanged.

In the reverse complementary invocation, advance the original D0->D_Uperp
with rank h-r, copy the complete stream, transform the copy to D1 with
rank r, perform all early scatter reads at common D1, and erase the copy.
The original already has its required inverse-mixer frame. Every scalar
center returns to its arbitrary old value under the transparent wrapper.

Thus the center return profile is `(r,h-r)` for Pij with r=h-2 and
Dij with r=h. No target-line containment in Uij is needed at the scatter:
the paid copy is explicitly in the common target background. Rebuilding
an intermediate Gi frame would be a different implementation with extra
costs, and is not assumed here. Each retained center must still have a
distinct output-use carrier with no intervening producer consumer; a
future complete compiler must enforce this rather than silently merge it.

This paid sequence justifies the component loss used in the optimistic
ledger if the ordinary two-stage side/middle program is supplied. It does
not supply that missing side program or establish the actual internal
histogram, tape guard, or full endpoint distribution.

Independent review by the coupled-transfer track accepts these common-frame
reads under the paid complete-stream interface, including both orientations.
It also found the denominator-256 scatter witness recorded above. The
five-subset construction must therefore derive a new tape guard and scalar
coefficient budget; the frozen PR36 seven-bit allowance is insufficient.

The tensor endpoint label now has weight 25, rather than the triple family's
weight 9. For the one-line phase T, the identity T squared = X_u leaves a
first-output correction F T to the power minus two. Its C inverse factors
occupy all 25 support axes. The conjugate Z_u and scalar i to the power
25f normalize F. Paid mask passes and the N width-one correction children
remain in the hypothetical ledger below. This identifies the required
endpoint semantics; a complete five-subset network and tape-guard replay
have not been supplied.

## Full optimistic characteristic envelope

Assume, without claiming it is implemented, that the old two-stage macro
and copied-center mechanism extends to five-subset norm-one labels.
Set v=binom(h,5), N=v^2, m=h^2 and local physical roles R. The complete
hypothesized ledger is

    W=2N+2vR,
    s=Wm-N+2vL0.

Its fixed external children are 2vR at width m-h, 2N at width (h-1)^2,
4N at width h-1 and N paid endpoint-correction children at width one.
The remaining internal rank mass is 2v(hR+L0), and local residual ranks
are at most h. Placing ALL that rank mass in width-h children minimizes
its moment for a positive saving b. This unrealistic but rigorous relaxation
gives a necessary role budget for any distribution satisfying this ledger.

## First discriminating side-cost assessment

The E4 family can use ordinary four-star leave-one-out circuits. For each
four-subset, q=h-4 possible fifth points need 3q-4 prefix/suffix additions.
There are 5v/q stars and 5v designated partial outputs. Before carrier
matching, this family alone costs `(20-20/q)*v` roles, about 18.75v at h=20.
Its source star has an orthonormal basis because common four-point
intersection is even; the excluded target output enters its binary kernel.

Naively constructing E2 separately for each pair gives ten weighted-triple
deletion families per original source. The measured triple D costs alone
are around 15-18 additions/source at these sizes, so a tenfold copy is
far beyond the roughly 32-role/source optimistic allowance. E0 adds
further cost. This direct construction is not a credible target route.

The continuation criterion is a side primitive jointly sharing E0/E2/E4
well enough to beat the envelope, or a changed phase architecture with a
new complete ledger. Exact scalar identities alone do not supply it.
No huge degree-five sweep has been launched.

A bounded four-worker follow-up built E0, every pair-conditioned E2 query
and E4 together, globally interning all equal ordinary supports. At h=10
it uses 67.675 unmatched side roles/source with pair blocks and 68.826
with triple blocks; at h=12 the respective costs are 98.276 and 136.925.
Every 0/2/4 intersection output is checked exactly. Centers and carrier
matching are excluded, and no common-frame feasibility is asserted.
These finite costs reject this direct scalar construction as the immediate
priority; they do not establish a lower bound for other side circuits.

Open obligations include a literal reversible side/gather/scatter schedule,
arbitrary dirty restoration with common frames, all local and endpoint
residuals, scalar guard and precision charges, source/sink semantics,
paid basis conversions, exact child distribution and all-size transfer.

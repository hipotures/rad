## Canonical pairing correction

The review of version two found a concrete boundary: guard placement
gives different within-band label orders. At f=4, the action order is
`(0,2,1,3)`, while the control orders are `(2,3,0,1)` and `(1,0,3,2)`.
Classifying the two placed controls at equal placed indices therefore
changes their matching. Version two is correct for its declared changed
matching, but its own reference cannot certify the original same-column
Gaussian row gate. Its original source and completed runs are retained.

Version three reconstructs both canonical control vectors by their
separately tracked label orders. Let o be the action label order and p
the stable canonical compaction permutation. Its placed action route is

    i -> o^-1(p(o(i))).

Returning the stock layout consequently gives precisely p in canonical
coordinates. After the stage alignment P, the first control band is
P^-1 times its previous value. Applying P in metadata recovers that
previous control, then its own label order recovers the canonical
column labels. The second control remains unchanged and is reconstructed
through its own order. These are metadata calculations, not an asserted
free permutation of coefficient records. The version-three negatives
explicitly reject omission of either control decoding or label recovery.

## Restored full-slot chronology

The six whole-band shears implement `(P action, P^-1 control1)`.
The first three are an ordinary exchange; the next three are the
P/P^-1 exchange. Each XOR mask reads only an immutable donor inside that
individual eight-rotation word. The complete second control band supplies
an arbitrary companion and is restored after each word.

The aligned switch stage consists of two interleaved three-XOR exchanges
on action quarters. Each reads its donor and the two restored external
controls, never its target or borrowed quarter. Those masks remain
unchanged throughout the individual native word. Undoing the alignment
restores both complete control bands at the stage endpoint. Reversing
the stage chronology gives the true inverse because the controls, and
hence the selected switch pattern, remain fixed.

The physical route acts only on the gapped active selected positions.
Every quarter has a real last-K guard chunk. Whole-band masks are zero
on its three internal guard positions, and its terminal guard is outside
the allowed selected set. Quarter masks also exclude the terminal guard.
The literal modular rotations may disturb complete guard values
temporarily; their full exceptional-record correction restores the
declared endpoint for arbitrary values. The finite array correction is
not itself a paid tape implementation. Its native cost requires the
unchanged-target predicate, bad-set invariance, full record keys,
complete payload sorting and reinsertion of the retained contract.

## All-size stock bound

The canonical cube has three f-chunk bands and twelve additional real
guard chunks, all within one complete role. They are existing address
axes, not twelve clean coefficient banks or a waived part of the target
primitive. Before inserting guards, the expected role-r interval is
shifted by 4r and ends 4(r+1) beyond its canonical band. At most
`4+8+12=24` active positions therefore have the wrong band role.
Twelve guard swaps affect at most twenty-four additional positions,
giving at most forty-eight wrong active positions. Every correcting
swap uses two still-wrong positions, fixes at least its chosen target,
and preserves the correctly placed positions. Thus at most twelve plus
forty-eight full-K swaps suffice for every supported f. Within-band
orders remain tracked rather than silently sorted.

The present Benes skeleton requires a power-of-two f>=4. For a general
parent width, padding with clean address axes is not authorized. A
separate remainder or non-power-of-two construction is required. Also,
the Gaussian gate acts on the original 3f selected axes and fixes the
twelve guard axes; a supplier for the whole parent zeta must separately
pay their endpoint transforms and include them in its child ledger.

## Gaussian and complete-chunk binding

For fixed full control addresses, selected-action compaction fixes every
unselected guard bit and merely permutes action selected bits. If a
columns are active, they occupy the first a regular action positions.
Fixing all controls and the high inactive action chunks leaves the
complete low aK-bit cube free. Unmoved guards are independent spectator
coordinates and need not follow the original selected bit to certify
that full cube. Different full prefixes form disjoint cylinders.

On each cylinder the physical row gate is

    G(c)^tensor a = diag(c^weight) Z_a diag(c^-weight).

The order of the two gauges and the true inverse G(-c) check. The
Gaussian producer uses c=1,-1,i,(1+i)/2; all these and their inverses
belong to the Gaussian-dyadic ring. Its full kernel formula preserves
the high inactive action coordinates and the subset relation of the
low active coordinates. Its direct physical formula uses the same
conditions in the original active positions. The version-three route
maps them bijectively, so the formulas agree coefficient by coefficient.

The producer's finite Gaussian binding uses its exact Boolean endpoint
word, not a second execution of every modular rotation. It checks every
selected source/target entry for each fixed control pattern, and records
one seeded full spectator/guard plane per complete control fiber. The
literal endpoint identity supplies the algebraic lift to all spectator
planes. This is distinct from independently enumerating the full
`2^((3f+12)K)` cube. Arbitrary dirty Gaussian values and the inverse are
checked, with explicit input/child/output grids and omitted-gauge
negatives. These finite producer counts are inspected evidence, not
measurements generated by this review.

## Native and asymptotic scope

There are `18(2 log2(f)-1)` packed XOR words in one compaction direction.
Their complete slot lengths are `(f+4)K` and `(f/4+1)K`; their guard-bound
correction fractions must use those declared lengths, including the
internal guards counted by the implementation. With A the actual
address/record metadata bound, the unsimplified conditional bill keeps
complete terms of the form

    sum_words [ V(L_word^tau+1) + M A^3
                + delta_word M A(R+A) ],

plus the paid full-K stock swaps, coefficient weight passes, dispatch,
child calls, the inverse direction and all original spectator records.
The masks may be computed in polynomial metadata time, but that does
not make a payload permutation free. Logarithmic stage count and
metadata scratch are explicit. This source review accepts the stated
composition only under the complete native contracts.

The width distribution remains binomial on the full control cube.
Ordinary twelve-gate Z3 has a neutral first moment and cannot establish
a saving by this activity mechanism alone. A real shorter complete word
would be a new input, and its recurrence also requires routing exponent
tau no larger than the chosen recursion exponent. A favorable optimistic
moment is therefore not a multiplier theorem.

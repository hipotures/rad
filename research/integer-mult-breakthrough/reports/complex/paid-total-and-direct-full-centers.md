## Paid total root

There are v source helpers and q+1 independent root seeds: R=v+q+1 permanent
center auxiliary roles. Each helper captures the unchanged original source

independent dirty root seeds.

For target T, the pair decoder coefficient is

    alpha_ij = 1/4 [ij subset T] - 3/32 ([i in T]+[j in T]),

and the total coefficient is 3/8. If t=|S intersect T|, the complete decoder is

    3/8 + (1/4) binom(t,2) - (3/8)t = (t-1)(t-3)/8.

Pair coefficients are 1/16, -3/32, or zero. Their common grid is 1/32; there is
no new odd divisor. A target touches exactly 5h-20 pair roots plus the total
root. At p12 the decoder therefore reads 101 roots per target, while the
pair-only substituted decoder may read all 264 pair roots. Each source writes
ten pairs and the total: eleven unit additions per M pass. Four conservative
M passes cost 44v scalar additions, and both decoder signs retain all reads.

The old scatter explicitly retains the total seed and all helper ancestors.
The late complete owned full-stream inverse/scatter cancels these dirty
responses. Originals stay full, the full M inverse restores every root, and
the source uninject follows any required K inverse. An omitted old total
response is a retained failing control.

## Direct-full variant and its profile

After the old M/read/M inverse at identity and all helper injections at source
lines, move every root 0->full and every helper line->full. Execute all late M
gates at the identical actual C_full representative. Read each root through a
complete owned copy transformed full->0. The original roots stay full for M
inverse and eventual source cleanup. The original source is still at its line
while these center steps run.

For one core the complete center histogram is

    multiplicity(1)=v, multiplicity(h-1)=v,
    multiplicity(h)=2(q+1).

It has rank hR+(q+1)h. One full-root copy costs h, not zero; it is proper only
under the retained master width 3h. A closed original descent/return costs
2(q+1)h instead. Scalar additions do not identify different actual operators.

The growing-frame variant has the same rank and stock. Concentrating the
helper traversal h-1 and each root growth h weakly lowers every concave width
moment: splitting a paid width a+b into a,b increases a^sigma+b^sigma for
0<sigma<1. This comparison concerns these exact two schedules and does not
price a missing side circuit.

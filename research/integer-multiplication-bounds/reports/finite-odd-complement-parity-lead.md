# A scoped complement-frame direction on odd grounds

This is an algebraic research lead, not a new finite witness or exponent
claim. The accepted h53 graph, checker and source frames are unchanged.

For a node with common core C and vertex union V, let W be the complement
of V. Define T(C,W) as the rational span of all triple indicators
`e_p+e_a+e_b`, with p in C and distinct a,b in W. These are all structurally
allowed downstream target triples meeting V in exactly their common point.
The old positive envelope E(C,V) is orthogonal to T(C,W), so it is contained
in U(C,V)=T(C,W)^perp for the ambient form `H=I-J/9`.

Whenever `C_child` contains `C_parent` and `V_child` is contained in
`V_parent`, the allowed target set grows towards the child. Consequently
`T_parent` is contained in `T_child` and `U_child` is contained in `U_parent`.
This gives a possible larger frame family while preserving target
orthogonality. Those set conditions must be checked on every actual frame
and retained interval; they must not be inferred from a formal source
support after delayed cloning.

Write r=|C|. For |W| at least three, the complete graph edge incidence on W
spans all coordinate vectors on W. The anchor zero-sum directions are a
positive orthogonal summand of dimension r-1. On the remaining summand,
with outside vector z, the anchor coordinates all equal `(sum z)/(2r)`.
The restricted form is

`||z||^2 - ((r-1)/(4r))*(sum z)^2`.

Thus T, and hence U in a nondegenerate ambient space, is degenerate only
at outside size `4r/(r-1)` for r>1. For r=2 this size is eight; for r=3 it
is six. The latter corresponds to the excluded ambient ground h=9 for
an input triple. For r=1 the target span is positive and never degenerates.
For |W|=2 the target generators have Gram matrix I+J and are nondegenerate;
the dimension formula from the complete nonbipartite graph does not apply.

A potentially discriminating odd-ground question is whether the actual
paired DAG's core-two unions always have even size. If that property held,
an odd h would make their outside size odd and would remove the size-eight
singularity. **That parity property has not been established here.**
Leave-one corrections may create core-two unions of odd size, so the first
next test should count actual core-two union sizes in a small original DAG,
rather than launch a full indefinite-frame search.

This differs from the earlier bounded negative in
[one-common complements](finite-one-common-complements.md), which only
broadened one-common-point frames on a fixed older h50 graph. It also
differs from the positive core-constrained completion exclusions in
[target frames](finite-target-frames.md). Neither negative establishes
performance or failure of the whole allowed-target complement family on
the changed odd-ground graph.

Any continuation needs exact restricted-form nondegeneracy, the input-copy
line contract, every forward and complementary reverse inclusion, all
designated target orthogonalities, literal operation and rank counts,
arbitrary dirty-scratch and stage-transfer controls. No role improvement is
implied by a larger admissible frame alone.

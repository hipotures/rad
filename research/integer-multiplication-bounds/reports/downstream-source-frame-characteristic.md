This branch applies the idea to the campaign's different independently audited triple-label program and its constructively fixed generic metric basis. It does not import the upstream ternary producer, controlled basis, dependency-path guard, or a new all-rank complex construction.

Every middle side and central role begins at `D0` of dimension `h^2-h` and ends at `D1` of dimension `h^2`. Assigning source frame `P_D0` and sink frame `I+P_D0` removes the entrance residual and changes the exit from `I-P_D1` to `I+P_D0-P_D1`. Since `D0` is a nondegenerate subspace of `D1`, the latter is the complementary projector of a nondegenerate dimension-`h` kernel. Its generic profile has one run `m-2h`, together with `h` singleton pivots. The total rank and `W`, `s`, `D` are unchanged. Actual endpoint eligibility, dirty restoration and simultaneous basis compatibility must be checked; a rank formula alone is insufficient.

The exact screen replaces only the middle grouped run. Joined and stage-three data runs remain unchanged, and every other residual remains individual. It checks

`D-a*(s*ln(m)_upper-M_lower)-a^2*s*ln(m)_upper^2/[2*(1-a*ln(m)_upper)] > 0`

with outward rational logarithm intervals.

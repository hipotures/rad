# Independent review: delayed clones with first-consumer frames

The delayed-clone mechanism has a valid all-size capacity, frame,
invocation and rank transfer. A clone may use a strictly larger positive
envelope than the canonical envelope of its formal sources, when that
envelope is the existing frame of the first consumer of one complete
moved controller chain. Equality of the clone's frame with its formal
core/support envelope is not required by the primitive.

For the recorded h51 candidate, the independent thin
[checker](../code/review_descendant_clone.py) accepts all 16,454 explicit
edit premises, exported link/placement identities, actual rank histogram,
role count and literal scalar guard. Its
[run](../runs/20261008T0550Z-review-descendant-clone/protocol.json) and
[certificate](../runs/20261008T0550Z-review-descendant-clone/results/certificate.json)
are frozen. This checker imports no clone builder or flow solver and
rebuilds no giant graph. Candidate promotion still needs the root-owned
independent changed-DAG/formal-source/actual-frame/compiled-hash audit.
The new role count 485,680 is therefore not promoted by this report alone.

## All-size construction and chronological premise

Let an original addition P=A+B have unused controller capacity and at
least two distinct output-controller chains. Choose one complete chain,
whose first consumer is G. Write `F=frame(G)`. Since P is an input of G,

`actual_span(P) subset frame(P) subset F`.

Both original child frames are also contained in frame(P). Choose an
original gate Q which uses the same formal operand B, whose controller
capacity is unused, whose assigned frame is contained in F, and whose
execution precedes G. Q may execute after P. Put an explicit identical
addition P' immediately before G, assign frame(P')=F, and move the whole
chosen output chain from P to P'. The first consumer and every subsequent
consumer retain their old frames.

The existing selected links on a controller chain give nested successive
consumer frames. Consequently every later consumer on the moved chain
contains F. Retain all original links, changing the formal source of
every use on the entire moved chain to P'. Add the two links from P's
use of A and Q's use of B to the corresponding two inputs of P'. These
links have exactly equal formal operands, distinct recipients, valid
chronology and nested frames.

P and Q had no retained inputs before the edit, and acquire at most one
each. Both new clone input uses terminate: no original successor starts
from a newly created input-use descriptor. The clone therefore has a
terminal pivot input, as do P and Q. Distinct clone jobs must consume
disjoint original gate capacities. The conservative batch filter also
forbids a chosen parent from being a formal child of another chosen
parent. These premises prevent ambiguous moved operands, conflicting
chronology or reuse of one scalar slot as two unrelated values.

Reindex originals in their old frame-dimension/node-ID order. Insert a
clone, with its owner's dimension, immediately before that owner. Children
and the two predecessor gates are already executed; every moved consumer
is still later. Multiple clones sharing one owner are placed in a fixed
order. Original input lines remain the first v IDs. The recovered program
then regenerates every descriptor and checks chronology using the actual
order; raw original IDs are not used as a substitute for that order.

## Positive frames, complements and direct targets

F is an existing positive envelope `E(C,V)`. It has positive restricted
rational form and is nondegenerate even when the whole ambient metric
`I-J/9` is indefinite. It contains both clone child frames and its actual
formal source span. At the first gate, the clone and owner use the same F;
at every later gate, F is contained in that existing gate's frame. All
unchanged input/output paths retain their former containments.

The ambient form is nondegenerate for h not equal to9. Taking orthogonal
complements therefore reverses every containment. The same actual frame
assignment gives the reverse cleanup path; there is no new binary/rational
field identification or characteristic-two nondegeneracy claim.

Direct output targets need special care. A chain starting with a direct
output uses the old source frame, not a larger arbitrary descendant frame.
If a gate-started old chain later ends at a direct output of P, its last
consumer frame must be contained in the old output frame(P). Combined
with `frame(P) subset F` and all forward chain containments, this forces
F=frame(P). Thus a strictly enlarged first-gate frame cannot feed such a
direct output through a valid old selected chain. All downstream gates
retain their old frames, so their old target orthogonality and the new
input containment imply orthogonality along those paths. The complete
changed program must nevertheless explicitly check every actual designated
target and its assigned physical frame.

The independent exact negative control shows why that check cannot be
discarded. In the h12 metric, let U be the line of triple012 and let F
be the span of triples012 and013. Their Gram matrices are [2] and
`[[2,1],[1,2]]`, both positive. U is contained in F. The target triple256
is orthogonal to U, but its pairing with triple013 is -1. Merely choosing
a positive larger frame containing the source would therefore invalidate
this direct target. By contrast target056 is orthogonal to all of F.
The same exact projector test verifies forward inclusion, reversed
complement inclusion and a rank-one positive residual.

## Scalar and arbitrary-dirty invocation

P' has exactly the same formal addition A+B as P. Substituting P' for P
on a complete output chain preserves every coefficient, while moving only
part of a chain can leave old links joining unequal formal sources. The
new network is cancellation-free over any scalar field, and its physical
copy/add schedule remains triangular and invertible on dirty auxiliary
values. The transparent mixer identity

`J L(a+Vx)-J L(a)=J L Vx`

does not depend on a label being the canonical formal-support envelope.
It depends on the exact mixer L, the valid common-frame assignment and
the source/target map. The central correction and the first/third bank
matching remain unchanged. No truncation or clean-scratch premise is added.

The recorded new h12 producer control contains a separately executed
independent rational timeline and all 4,126 complete source/target/central/
dirty basis vectors in each orientation. Its 4,114-coordinate side basis
excludes the 12 central registers. The checker validates both orientations
and those exact fields; it does not describe them as a new giant replay.

## Role, rank, literal guard and changed histogram

One extra addition creates two new links. Thus

`R_new=(c+1)+q-(links+2)=R_old-1` per compatible clone.

For the h51 record, `c=489563`, `q=62475`, `links=66358`, and R=485680.
All 16,454 clone frames are strictly larger in the supplied edit metadata;
all selected insertion owners are nonterminal. The thin audit checks
32,908 distinct original gate capacities, compatible parent/child sets,
unique new clone IDs, placement before mapped owners, distinct predecessor
and recipient link descriptors and increasing exported-use indices.
Actual frame objects and gate capacity identities are separate premises
checked by the independent giant reconstruction.

For nested nondegenerate U and V, `P_V-P_U` is the projection onto
`V intersect U^perp`, of rank `dim(V)-dim(U)`. Hence the sum along each
monotone physical-role path telescopes independently of intermediate
envelope dimensions. The forward and complement middle passes still
sum to R h per local invocation. Cleanup ends at the same full ground
frame. Shared join and middle-bank endpoint frames, source corrections,
data endpoints and central ranks are unchanged. Therefore

`W=2N+2v²(R+h)`, `L=3v²h²`, `D=N-2L`, `s=Wm-D`.

The independent checker sums the complete changed zero/positive histogram
and every category, rather than substituting the old middle histogram.
It accepts `W=439367045355000` and `s=58282475670006923125`, with
`D=2263379181875`. Grouped middle/joined/data boundary families may use
these counts in a fresh characteristic, but an optimization depending on
individual internal rank frequencies must consume this new histogram.

The scalar operation count is also literal. For c additions and R roles,
the forward mixer has `c+R-v` operations. One whole invocation uses four
mixers and the original source, target and central operations, giving
`4(c+R-v)+20v`. Added sums replace equally many physical copies, so this
quantity is unchanged by each profitable clone. Nevertheless the new
W changes the guard arithmetic and it is recomputed. The exact checked
values are

`E=5428282150343310471226823308052812797319155712`,

`E-(2GW²+4s+4W+4)=3301393637375458038560503238883475024110043208>0`.

The separate complex primitive's arithmetic/guard table is unaffected.
Any changed fixed rational address table still uses the usual finite
bad-prime exclusion and eventual setup conditions; no new explicit
alphabet constant is claimed here.

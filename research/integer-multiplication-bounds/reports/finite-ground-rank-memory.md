# Actual role counts across even grounds and a measured memory failure

The question was whether a different ground improves the supported bit
saving under the new compact recurrence. Every successful case retains the
complete global cancellation-free scalar check, physical coefficient/frame
compiler, designated-target orthogonality, exact matching and physical edge
rank histogram. The ranking uses the rigorously enclosed saving
-log(1-D/(Wm))/log(m), not raw roles or the prospective rank-batching score.

The original [rank-ground cohort](../runs/20261008T032050Z-finite-rank-ground-cohort/report.md)
evaluated grounds h40 through h58 with a 6 GiB address-space cap per worker.
It preserved 176 exact successful candidates before 36 MemoryError records
and 16 BrokenProcessPool records ended the pool. Its original source,
checkpoint, errors and logs are retained. A wrapper failure and partial
scientific success are recorded separately; missing cases are not negatives.

The fresh [repair cohort](../runs/20261008T034200Z-finite-rank-ground-repair/protocol.json)
excludes every completed original ID. It evaluates 61 formerly unverified
cases at h<=52 and 280 distinct endpoint-window cases at h38 through h52.
One fresh process per candidate prevents previous graph/caches from affecting
the next case. All scalar/frame checks remain the frozen evaluator's checks.
It completed 306 verified candidates without a memory failure. The other 35
cases have h38 and fail the required positive network deficit: C(38,3)=8436
is below 6*38^2=8664, so D=v^2(v-6h^2)<0 regardless of compiled roles.
The successful wrapper exit therefore covers a terminal scientific cohort
with these explicit rejected cases, rather than 341 positive certificates.

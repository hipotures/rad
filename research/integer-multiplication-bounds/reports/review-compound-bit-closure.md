Let the current node P have actual operands A and B. P must have unused

The actual assignments imply

`frame(A), frame(B) subset frame(P) subset F`.

Choose a current gate Q which consumes the same actual operand B, has
unused retention capacity, executes before G, and has its actual frame

before G and assign F to P'. Move every use on the chosen controller chain

P's use of A and Q's use of B to the corresponding two P' inputs.

These two links join the same formal values, have distinct recipient uses,
and respect both chronology and actual frame containment. P and Q each

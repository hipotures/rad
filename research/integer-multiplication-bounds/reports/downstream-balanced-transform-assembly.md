## Strict parameters and arithmetic

The [exact adapter](../code/downstream_balanced_transform_assembly.py)
regresses every previous compact parameter, recurrence, margin, strict
slack and cutoff, then recomputes the changed construction. It preserves
two rational parameter rows. Write a=1-tau, b=1-sigma, h for the fixed
backoff and x=1-beta. The choices are

```
c=1-h, q=a(1-2h), x=(q/b)(1+h), zeta=h,
epsilon=(1-h)/(1+c), r=(1-epsilon)/2, delta=r/8.
```

The conservative backoff is h=a/16 and the tight backoff is h=2^-64.
All 37 saved inequalities are strict and checked with exact fractions.
The new prefix margin is 1-epsilon. The condition
1-epsilon(1+c)=h remains positive and ensures K=o(ell), but it need not
exceed kappa. The negative control confirms that the old prefix estimate
would reject the saved witness. The guard retains
C1=1+4x+zeta and the complete finite-dependent C0.

The tight common numeric cutoff is

```
log2(b_input) >= 55340232221128654848 = 3 * 2^64.
```

This is a finite asymptotic cutoff, dominated by the nearly limiting
geometry choice. At this cutoff ell/K>=b_input^h/2>=4, so every chunk
fits with the retained size bounds. The conservative R473026 row uses
common cutoff 15,083,685,472. Gamma normalization, logarithmic alpha,
full guard, phase-cell size and compact-field lower bounds have their
own recorded exact cutoffs. Prime existence, strict logarithm absorption,
polynomial record domination and the initial padded-box bounds retain
separate eventual thresholds; the displayed number alone does not claim
to make every asymptotic absorption true.

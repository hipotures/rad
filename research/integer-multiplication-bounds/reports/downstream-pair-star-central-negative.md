# Pair-star identity and a sharp scoped central-rank negative

The proposed pair labels and scalar identity are exact. A cheaper central
schedule exists, but even its minimum rank charge prevents a saving in the
declared three-stage motif. This is a targeted algebraic discriminator,
not an exclusion of different data-label chronology or another scalar
representation.

Let S range over the v = binom(h,2) pairs of an h-point set. In binary
augmented coordinates set `t_S = e0 + sum_(a in S) e_a`. Each line has
norm one, and

```
t_S dot t_T = 1 + |S intersect T| mod 2.
```

Thus lines of two distinct pairs sharing one point are orthogonal. If E1
is the incidence matrix for intersection exactly one, then over the
rational scalar domain

```
I_(S,T) = |S intersect T|/2 - E1_(S,T)/2.
```

The central part uses h point-incidence registers. E1 is the sum of two
star-minus-self partial outputs. The usual early subtraction, copy and
gather, late addition, undo gather and undo copy restores arbitrary
scratch and effects `y <- y+x`. The small reference tests include the
center registers in every dirty basis probe. They do not compile a new
large positive side graph.

The pair-label span has dimension h. For odd h its bilinear form is
nondegenerate, with orthonormal basis
`z_j = e0 + sum_(k != j) e_k`. In this basis
`t_{a,b} = sum_(j not in {a,b}) z_j`. For even h, the pair span has the
one-dimensional radical generated by the ground-coordinate all-ones
vector. Its quotient is nondegenerate of dimension h-1; the star at point
zero is an orthonormal basis. Every point-star has h-1 orthonormal labels
in either model. These are favorable ambient dimensions, smaller than
the initial h+1 augmented coordinates.

There is a useful schedule improvement for the center registers. Gather
all centers with the full local frame. In the late scatter, handle each
target pair S={j,k} by one joint gate on its target and the two centers,
with common frame `H_S=t_S^perp`. The target is then already in its final
kernel. A center at j visits h-1 such kernels. Consecutive target lines
on that center are orthogonal, so their relative kernel has rank two.
The first full-frame-to-kernel change and the final return each have
rank one. Each center's closed path therefore has rank

```
1 + 2(h-2) + 1 = 2(h-1).
```

The effective local decrease is h(h-1) for all h centers. At odd h this
saves h relative to the inherited full-frame return charge h^2. It also
avoids transferring an additional loss onto the target wires.

This center charge is minimal in the declared monotone-target family.
Here is a general exact rank lemma. Let symmetric matrices Delta_i sum
to zero, and let R be the span of all their images. Then

```
sum_i rank(Delta_i) >= 2 dim(R).
```

To prove it over any field, factor each rank-r_i symmetric matrix as
`U_i G_i U_i^T`, where U_i has full column rank r_i and G_i is invertible
and symmetric. Concatenate the U_i and use the nondegenerate block form
`G=diag(G_i)`. The identity `U G U^T=0` says that the row space of U is
totally isotropic. Its dimension is dim(R), so it is at most half the
dimension of G, namely half of sum r_i. This also proves the statement
in characteristic two, including alternating blocks.

Apply the lemma to one center's closed projection path from the full
frame after gather to the full frame before undo gather. At a scatter
touching target {j,k}, its common projector annihilates t_{j,k}, since
that target's frame remains inside its final kernel. Therefore
`t_{j,k}=(I-P_current)t_{j,k}` lies in the span of the preceding matrix
differences' images. All h-1 orthonormal star directions lie in R. The
center path costs at least 2(h-1) ranks, attained by the joint schedule
above. This bound allows other intermediate symmetric projectors and
scatter batches; its scope retains the full gather/undo frames and
monotone target-kernel condition.

The three tensor stages have 3v^2 invocations and N=v^3 data roles. Even
granting zero additional side losses and the full 2N endpoint saving,

```
L >= 3 v^2 h(h-1),
D <= 2N-2L = -5 v^2 h(h-1) < 0.
```

This negative is independent of the side circuit role count. Sharing a
restored scratch bank removes a full-rank boundary and a role together,
and does not repair the deficit. A changed chronology that lets target
frames leave their final kernels would require a fresh total-rank audit;
it is not covered by the center-only lower bound.

## Changed obligations and current review

The written interface uses complete M-record streams, each record of R bits,
with V=MR and address descriptions A=ceil(log2(2V)). A binary-controlled
rotation has at most M prefix fibers. Descriptor work `M*A^C` can be paid
by the record volume only because R is superpolynomial in p and A=O(p).
The tape argument must avoid reading the growing descriptor for every
payload bit; the original amortized counter and stream contract are retained.

Three dirty complete temporary fields hold separated low digits. The
four-update identity changes the selected address bits while restoring
the scratch. Front/back swaps place each rotation control before its target,
including the case where the original source field is later in the address.
The highest selected bit is excluded from the carry guard and handled by a
separate elementary XOR.

With G=4*ceil(log2 p)+6, n<=p and K>=G+4*ceil(log2 p)+10, the displayed
bad-address bound satisfies
`n*(2*2^-G+8*2^(G-K)) <= 5/(128*p^3)`.
On the complement of the bad set the modular construction agrees with the
ideal XOR. Since the latter preserves that set and both maps are bijective,
the constructed permutation preserves it too. Exceptional records can be
repaired and stably returned using address keys. Their O(p^-3) density must
be charged locally to each invocation's volume; the old globally
exponentially sparse repair argument is not applicable here.

Complete front/back temporary fields are carved from preprocessed axes,
without adding address coordinates. A row index, excluding all temporary
bits, is padded to a multiple of W^k0. Each split `u=W*g+w` produces W
complete roles of equal volume V/W. The spectator prefix g may have a
nonbinary cardinality; the original chunk-swap contract explicitly admits
arbitrary positive rectangular prefix, gap and suffix cardinalities.
Temporary fields must be restored at every scalar gate, child return and
completed call. These requirements are part of the mathematical interface.

The changed normalized internal exponent is
`chi=tau+(1-beta)*max(sigma-tau,0)`; leaf cost has exponent
`sigma+beta*(1-sigma)`, and reservations have exponent `max(1-c,0)`.
Coefficient growth remains attached to scalar gates, not exact address
permutations. The stopped-depth bound is `5-4*beta+zeta`. The distinct
complex network supplies its own m,W,s, while the bit routine supplies only
its swap exponent and tape contract. Equality of bit and complex arities is
not required.

Independent exact address tests have passed 139,264 complete small
rectangle addresses and 207,360 adversarial carry/guard endpoints. They
include both source orders, inverse repair, highest-bit completion and
complete-field row splits. Independent written movement, layout, coefficient guard and complex
interface review is positive. The assembly review separately reconstructs
738 stopped recurrences, independent count/logarithm bounds and every strict
condition. These controls support transfer within the retained conditional
contracts; they do not establish an unconditional multiplication theorem.

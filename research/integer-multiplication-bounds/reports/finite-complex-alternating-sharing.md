# Alternating residuals and exact D/E helper sharing

The reviewed alternating-quadratic interface admits arbitrary nested
nondegenerate binary frames. A rank-r alternating interval uses r ordinary
f-axis complex children and the paid compact adapters, with at most 4s extra
elementary coefficient charges. It does not provide free gathers or a
variable-shrink child. The fresh finite implementation keeps the old
nonalternating checker frozen and explicitly checks every actual alternating
residual projector, symplectic basis, quadratic coefficients, Arf sign,
reverse-complement interval and scalar phase. Complete changed scalar,
physical, target and endpoint checks remain mandatory.

For the actual compiled graph, the guard is
`2 G W^2 + 8 s + 4 W + 4 < E`. G is computed from literal scalar gates and
clone count. The old shortcut G<6W is not used. Fresh entries and terminal
complements remain nonalternating in this family.

Cross-D/E sharing identifies exact identical formal support sums. The E pair
frame is nondegenerate and contained in the original D coordinate frame.
Even outside cardinality gives a nonalternating strict residual; odd outside
cardinality gives an alternating strict residual. The older even-only source
records the odd cases as exclusions. The fresh all-pair source uses the
reviewed alternating interface and the actual physical checker, so it can
include those four odd helpers at h8/h12 without relying on endpoint types
alone. DAG IDs are rebuilt explicitly, and every changed coefficient is
reconstructed from gate arguments.

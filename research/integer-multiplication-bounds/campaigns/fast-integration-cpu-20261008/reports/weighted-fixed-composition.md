## Joint pool optimization and scope

At a fixed saving a, let f_a(t)=t*(m/t)^a. For axis h, let c_h(t) denote its
adjusted internal blocks plus its exterior blocks and let B_h be its wire
bank. Then the numerator minus mW is

`constant(a) + sum_h [sum_t c_h(t)*f_a(t) - m*B_h]`.

The data, growth and paid endpoint contribution is independent of the two
local profiles. Thus the minimum over a Cartesian pair pool equals the sum
of the two separate axis minima. [joint_profile_pool.py](../code/joint_profile_pool.py)
uses rational log/exponential enclosures and outward2^-128 weights. Both
lower and upper minima bound the true minimum; an overlap aborts rather than
claiming an exact bracket. It checks equality with the full complete
controller at a=0 and at the final positive saving.

The first frozen pool covers 512 h23 and 446 h25 profiles, or 228352 pairs,
and selects the original h23 plus the R49614 h25 variant at
a=402683228057/10^16. This excludes better pairs only inside that frozen
pool under its fixed matching choices. It does not prove global optimality.
The matrix-weighted lane improves that result independently. The now-complete
original profile pool and growing weighted pool are queued for new frozen
optimizations.

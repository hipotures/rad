# Complete recurrence comparison for shared pair producers

Twenty-nine coherent physical profile combinations were evaluated against
κ=5.14028687e-5. Every composition passed exact bit and complex moments,
47 strict assembly constraints and seven margins. None improved the accepted
conditional exponent. The strongest adverse matching control has
κ=5.1389472e-5 at the same W=136283234; producer variants pay additional roles
and perform worse in the tested allocations.

The experiments include paid shared pair sums of lengths4 and8, a full-star
chain, recipient antichain variants, and a least-gain-first matching control.
Each actual native profile and word digest is checked against independent
literal source reconstruction before composition. Only beta1/15 at dimension23
and beta7/207 at dimension25 use the retained complete4073300 DATA certificate.
Eighteen other native basis profiles are deliberately omitted from these
comparisons pending their compatible DATA proofs.

This is a scoped negative result for the tested producer schedules and matching
policies. It excludes neither cross-common sharing with different frames nor
joint synthesis across newly merged regions. Since no candidate improves the
accepted score, full stock and all conditional acceptance checks are not claimed
for these rejected combinations. The source DAG and dirty word checks remain
separate retained evidence.

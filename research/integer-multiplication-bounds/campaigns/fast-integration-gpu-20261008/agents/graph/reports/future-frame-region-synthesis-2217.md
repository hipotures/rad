# Reachable output kernels and joint region synthesis

The experiment asks whether selecting rational frames from future output
obligations can merge scalar regions whose original producer envelopes differ.
The interval scalar DAG is pinned to Avi Eisenberg's PR #62 construction
`ad0f25ff7b23cff7f08ad237c2254e6ecf74257e`. Binary synthesis, carrier
capacities and explicitly paid reclamation are inherited from eumemic's
PR #57. This investigation was assisted by OpenAI Codex and retains the
public source's full attribution and license chain.

For a one-core region with common coordinate c, an ordinary target (c,a,b)
requires the additional equation x_a+x_b=0 inside the common hyperplane.
Backward unions of reachable target pairs define a signed bipartite graph:
bipartite connected components supply one signed free coordinate and odd
components force their coordinates to zero. The resulting positive frame
contains the original scalar envelope. Every scalar dependency is checked
for actual containment before compiling a new physical word. Source
injections, two-core frames and copied centers retain their original spaces.
There is no decreasing-frame operation or restriction to ridge source arrays.

The maximal signed space from all downstream physical events and terminal
kernels is already present in the inherited `joint_word_positive_closure`
construction. It is not a new enlargement theorem. The distinct experiment
here drops the old matching as a constraint, derives frames per original
scalar region, merges equal spaces, and reoptimizes the physical word.

Both dimensions used the already source-verified rank-first scalar parents

merged-region count nor a retained-link count is a sufficient objective.
These are measured negatives for the stated finite families; no general
impossibility of a different joint architecture is claimed.

The actual-word variant keeps the already verified changed matching word as
an immutable lower-space certificate, reconstructs its preintern region
mapping from source metadata, derives the inherited physical-event closure,
and recompiles after assigning the resulting spaces to scalar nodes. Its
completed h23 variants retain 27,719 roles and 48,889 regions. At this
granularity the full selected spaces remain distinct. Exact duplicate frame
assignments are skipped before another compilation. The fixed-word full
closure also worsens the complete local native moment at the selected basis;
it is not an improvement solely because it adds positive directions.

The current follow-up computes exact ordered CRT matrix costs on the genuinely
merged scalar-future regions and reruns gain-first carrier selection. Every
selected use, donor row, recipient capacity and strict chronology is checked
again before constructing and profiling the actual paid word. Floating
moments order discovery only; they are not certificates of an exponent or
weighted optimality.

These finite word checks exhaust binary payload role directions. The
all-address assertion remains an algebraic transfer through the inherited
common-frame gauge schedule, subject to its physical realization and charges.
Actual matrix profiles, complete source-pair DATA and stock, recursive
moments, 47 strict assembly constraints and seven margins remain separate
acceptance gates. The dense free terminal transfer was independently
falsified under the inherited arbitrary-array contract and is not used here.

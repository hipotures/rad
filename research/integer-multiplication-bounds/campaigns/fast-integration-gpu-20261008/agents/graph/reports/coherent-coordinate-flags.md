# Coherent coordinate flags at fixed scalar graph

The question is whether a cheaper physical width moment can be obtained
without another scalar graph rewrite. The negative-basis and paired dual
experiments show that ordered matrix corner profiles depend on the chosen
physical coordinate flag even when role/rank accounting is unchanged.

Let Q be a coordinate permutation. Apply Q to every primitive source
triple, scalar core/cover mask, forced coordinate and signed positive-frame
coordinate. Keep logical node IDs, addition operands, designated output
incidences and chosen controller chains unchanged. Both endpoint source
and target labels receive that same permutation.

The address form G=I-J/9 and every common basis L_beta=I-beta J commute
with Q. A nondegenerate frame U therefore has projector
P_(QU)=Q P_U Q^-1; the same identity holds for differences of nested
projectors. Consequently all scalar coefficients, exact inclusions,
physical chronology and transition ranks transfer. The chosen controller
map remains valid, and every retained center is still a separate terminal
role with frame rank h-1. The finite-field Gram and minor conditions still
need their documented exact check.

The full family of three-element sources and center indices is invariant
under permutation. Thus the complete source/source, source/center and
center/center boundary matrix family is unchanged as a multiset at the
same beta values. This allows reuse of the unchanged complete data-family
certificate, including copied-center coordinate products. It does not
allow reuse of a different beta family's data profile. Internal projector
families are not permutation complete, and their ordered northeast corner
profiles are recomputed with bounded integer minors and enough primes.

The transformer verifies that inverse permutation recovers every DAG and
positive-label byte exactly, that the entire source triple family maps
bijectively, and that the selected-use map is unchanged. The versioned
literal checker recomputes the scalar source/target incidence from the
recorded permutation and checks both endpoint directions. Small reverse
and adjacent controls exercise all2,044 source, target and dirty role basis
vectors over F2; both passed. Fullh23 andh25 reversal audits also passed.
The first checker attempt failed because tuple representations of unordered
source triples were not sorted; that operational traceback was retained,
and a fresh repaired run passed. No construction invariant was relaxed.

The initial606 distinct flags on two scalar graphs all passed exact matrix
profiling. Identity reproduces the negative baseline, and reversal
reproduces the previously certified paired dual block histograms exactly.
Distant random permutations were more expensive in that finite cohort.

orders exclude every original flag. A nearbyh23 coordinate20/21 swap has
R36,219 and localPhi170.287781843347 at alpha4e-5, below the old global dual
localPhi170.31415115944. That number is a complete local profile candidate,
not an independently assembled multiplication exponent. The coordinator
owns exact full composition and strict absorption, and a fresh selected

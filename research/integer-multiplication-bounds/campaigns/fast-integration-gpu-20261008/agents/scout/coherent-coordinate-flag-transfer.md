# Coherent coordinate flags on the executed joint word

At the inherited physical DATA order, a global coordinate permutation can
change local NE profiles without changing the complete DATA family. The
permutation must also change the logical source and output labels. A local
matrix profile alone does not supply that interface.

Let `Q_h` permute the `h` common coordinates. It fixes the all-ones vector,
so it commutes with `H_0=I-J/9` and every `L_beta=I-beta J`. A source line,
its dual, and any common-basis frame projector therefore satisfy

`p_beta(T) -> p_beta(Q_h T)`,
`xi_beta(T) -> xi_beta(Q_h T)`,
`P_beta(C,U) -> Q_h P_beta(C,U) Q_h^-1`.

The same statement applies to enlarged signed frames when their signed
coordinate classes are relabeled together. A source normalization remains
one. Each center `i` becomes center `Q_h i`, preserving its normalized
primal/dual pairing, nonzero coordinate gates, full-cover terminal, copied
entrance and still-paid rank-one original cleanup. The complete triple and
center families are closed under this bijection.

The actual selected-word convention keeps the physical tensor permutation,
corner rows and corner columns fixed. For inverse source-product vectors
`x_beta(T)`, `y_beta(S)`, the normalized DATA matrix is

`M_beta(T,S)[(r,b),(c,d)] = delta_rc*x_beta(T)[r] + delta_bd*y_beta(S)[b] - 1`.

Consequently the flagged construction has

`M_flag(T,S) = M_old(Q_23 T,Q_25 S)`.

The lexical triple maps are permutations of 1,771 and 2,300 items. Their
Cartesian map covers all 4,073,300 source pairs exactly once, preserving
the complete DATA profile histogram. Entrywise equality at an unchanged
triple name is not asserted. No full-family matrix replay is necessary.

The serialized source-slot dictionary stays unchanged in the selected
23-coordinate word. Its key `k` is interpreted as the logical triple
`Q(T_k)` using the retained `source_label_permutation`. The independent
audit checks every first actual source frame against that interpretation,
every copied-center output, every changed frame mask and output label,
and equality of the unchanged scalar operations, scatter operations,
event IDs and physical role counts. It also binds the exact transition
bytes used by the geometry profiler.

For `Q_23=(20 21)`, this gate is closed by

using the graph team's literal source-aware word and the pinned PR62

The established DATA inputs transferred by this receipt are I+J/I+J,
I+J/negative25, half23/negative25 and half23/I+J. Their source and center

The selected local profiles still require their fresh exact bounded-minor

The entire XOR word and role allocation are unchanged, so its scalar

4,793,351,472. The bit recurrence pays this finite scalar schedule through

recursive width contractions, which are unchanged here.

This is a finite interface lemma and independent source audit. General
conditional lifting, compiler recovery, fresh local NE profiles and the
complete characteristic/semantic assembly remain separate obligations.

## Complete moment and actual register chains

`physical_sinks.py` constructs the substituted physical register chains
directly. Each sink write promotes the chosen target pivot and its control
port; the post-shears promote the pivot and the other targets at the actual
side-root frame. The model also includes time-zero and deferred dirty reads,
source injections, copied-center scatter, target corrections, the source
transform and literal full-frame inverse cleanup. Removed sink slots do not
appear in the chains. The control has exactly 13,894 registers and 93,183
recorded literal frame transitions.

For every final plan, the complete child histogram is counted from these
chains, copied-center children, unmerged gauge tails and `2v` ordinary leaves.
All three recursive stages are paid. The measured function is

`H(s) = sum_r n_r * r * (66/r)^s / (66 * W)`.

Each proposal prices every affected chain edge with its full multiplicity.
Joins propagate to successors and intersections to predecessors only where
nesting needs repair. Every operation value span and every boundary inclusion
is checked by exact F2 elimination. The final histogram must equal the pinned
native body checker followed by `sinks_gate.sinks_record`, including its full
forward/reflected frame scans, dirty modular replay and four rejected
controls. The full sink inventory, rather than the unsubstituted body saving,
selects the fd candidates.

`physical_sink_elementary.py` tests a further bounded family: one-dimensional
joins and codimension-one meets in the global feasible quotient, followed by
the same transitive nesting repairs. It samples at most four canonical quotient
basis directions per seed, 8,192 seeds per pass and two passes. It is neither
an exhaustive quotient orientation search nor a global optimality proof.

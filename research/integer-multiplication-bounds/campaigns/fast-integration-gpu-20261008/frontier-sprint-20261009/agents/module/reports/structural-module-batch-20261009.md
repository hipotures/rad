The initial triple census found 1,953 live gates and zero duplicate-support

## Whole-graph construction and checks

Every case reconstructs the whole graph from its explicit task-owned local
and triple JSON files. The native pair module, all-but-one module,
`f8:00111100` output fold and ordinary centers are retained. Source pins are
checked before execution and after completion. The original public source
was never patched.

The carrier constructor uses exact signed polynomial/operand/readout
correspondences to seed current native carrier choices on new node and root
IDs. It then admits further legal carriers while preserving unit
destination coefficients. It freshly rebuilds full dependency closure,
gauges and the chronological literal word. This is a semantically seeded
construction, not an independent maximum-matching optimum. Every operation
frame and physical alias is rebuilt from that word with four joint rounds,
up to six component sweeps, and the global lower-frame continuation at
budget 8,192, with seed 20261009.

Finite verification covers signed scalar H, K and its inverse, decoder
identity, source supports/itinerary, full backward intersections, literal
physical M, gauge intersections and reverse target chains. The independent
integer adjoint audit checks both shear orientations, every source
coefficient, arbitrary dirty auxiliary coefficients, unchanged target
spectators and exact cleanup. Omitted read, bad sign, illegal index and
omitted cleanup controls are all rejected.

Sink candidates and pivots are freshly enumerated for each actual graph,
word, aliases and frames. The native gate rechecks destination-only/no-gauge
eligibility, source/deferred-read exclusions, all literal and reflected
frame chains, and the complete per-class paid inventory. An independent
characteristic-zero symbolic star identity checks every sink, including
formal controls, target spectators and cancellation of the old dirty sink
variable. The native sink gate also retains its four stage-order/reflection
controls. Sink counts are actual target-backed substitutions; no rank-zero
proxy or profile splice is used.

The four-case attempt took about 68 seconds with four workers. The repaired
all-long case took about 70 seconds with one worker. Every process used one
library thread, standard-library Python and no GPU framework.

## Failure and repair

The first all-long case failed before producing a finite profile:

```text
AssertionError: ('frozen arc rejected', 1331, 2642)
```

Exact signed polynomial and operand/readout correspondence is insufficient
to establish carrier legality after a graph rewrite. New intermediate
spans, backward intersections or paths can invalidate the mapped seed.
The existing extension checker caught this rather than accepting a stale
carrier. The initial attempt, its three completed cases, source protocol
and complete failure log are preserved unchanged.

The distinct repair run rechecks each mapped seed sequentially against the
new accumulated spans, intersections, acyclicity and shear coefficient.
Rejected seeds do not mutate the matching state. It rejects 660 of the
all-long seeds before legal extension, then passes the whole finite body and
sink checks. The resulting complete paid histogram exactly equals the
all-edge histogram. The retained evaluator patch records this additional
validation; the failed case is not presented as an accepted construction.

The three positive-module negative fixtures independently confirm rejection
of overlapping operands, a forward operand and a wrong query root. Python
source syntax and the executable reproduction shell were checked.

## Interpretation and limits

Gate count and depth alone are poor objectives for this paid construction.
The edge-based local rewrite removes 330 gates overall but loses 660
carriers. Its register count and W rise by 330, while literal M is
unchanged. The long-diagonal rewrite reaches the same inventory after its
mapped-seed repair.

The positive triple rewrite removes 27 gates but loses 173 carriers, which
raises R by 146 and literal M by 119. It creates 32 additional valid sinks,
reducing the final W penalty to 114. The extra sink eligibility is a real
structural effect, but does not compensate for the carrier loss here.

These measurements compare a common bounded fresh physical constructor.
The unchanged control does not import the more optimized native public
physical frame plan. This is not a proof that every association or module
DAG is worse, nor an optimum certificate for matching or frames. It is
decisive negative evidence for the three implemented alternatives and their
specified construction protocol. Further broad local or unchanged-word
sweeps were stopped at the coordinator's instruction. A future structural
objective would need to preserve carrier reuse and measure the complete
sink-adjusted paid profile jointly.

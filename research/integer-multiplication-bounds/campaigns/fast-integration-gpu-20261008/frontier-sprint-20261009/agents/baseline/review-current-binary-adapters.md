# Current binary adapter inspections

These are completed **GRAPH_ONLY** and **SCALAR_ONLY** inspections of the changed
PR168 binary producer. They do not accept a new finite supplier or multiplication
exponent. The historical first-plan, native `98c115b`, and accepted paired-both
lifetime reviewer packages remain byte unchanged.

The independent graph adapter exactly reconstructs the inline output order in
`eumemic/integer-mult-bounds@91f6a059f44fb0513639d5185bde2a38973e99ca`,
then its L1 local associations in
`eumemic/integer-mult-bounds@fd25adb7fbaa12ee761d02c733c54d1d2a7687ee`.
All source identities, input hashes, recovery locations and reviewer dependency
hashes are in [the pin manifest](configs/current-binary-inspection-pins.json).
No upstream producer or search Python is imported by these inspections.

## Changed input scope

The `91f6a05` producer builds its u01/w02 sum nodes inline before the centers.
This differs from the accepted historical own construction, which appended its
fusion nodes after the original graph. IDs, root order, carriers, K delivery
anchors and frames therefore need fresh reconstruction.

The `fd25adb` producer additionally uses diagonal local L1 associations at
`{(0,1,0), (0,1,1), (1,2,1)}`. Its integer graph has 24,756 nodes and 6,624
roots, compared with 24,316 and 6,624 at `91f6a05`. Its public physical word
also defers copies. It exports operation frames, read cuts, aliases and late
copies, and omits native compiler arcs/order/plain metadata. The native carrier
checker cannot receive this physical word transparently. The separate scalar
adapter reads the actual word and reconstructs its physical slots and deadlines.

The post-native third fusion is a different operation from the inline fusion:
it appends 880 disjoint face0/edge12 sums after the complete native graph,
removes 440 four-target face0 roots, and replaces the existing edge12 roots
in place. Its 6,184 roots preserve every contribution once per receiver and
retain the original-source K mixer. The independently inspected integer graph
has 25,636 nodes. The final discovery fixture has 2,200 aliases, rather than
the 1,760 initially proposed. Those aliases were not independently accepted
by this graph inspection.

an omitted third fusion changes the actual graph and is rejected.

recipients. Moving an actual compensation read before the program rejects the
full F2 identity in both orientations. Source-pair, source-graph and reviewer

**SCALAR_ONLY** remains literal: this replay does not certify G-frame nesting,

a substitute for the complemented physical proof. In particular, no previous
finite receipt applies automatically to these changed inputs.

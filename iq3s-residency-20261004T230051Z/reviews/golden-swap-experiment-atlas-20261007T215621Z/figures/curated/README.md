For research discussion, prioritize physical slots, CURRENT/FULL_ORACLE, the paired Phase 1/2 expert-122 mechanism and the AUC/outcome view. Early/full churn and startup distinguish continuing turnover from an assumed startup-only correction. Repeated expert, demand and lease views support a future opportunity-cost question.

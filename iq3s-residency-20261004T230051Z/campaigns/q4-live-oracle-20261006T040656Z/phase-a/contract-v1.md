# Fixed-work contract v1 (development implementation)

The logical event is `(request, verifier-window index, role main/MTP, layer or draft-step, position, lane, routing-call group)`. One main routing event contains all `T x 10` entries. Entries within that invocation remain batched and are not serialized into scheduling events. Main layers 0–23 precede the second stage's 24–47. The accepted prefix is committed with the real commit graph; MTP catch-up and every recorded draft step execute afterwards, including work subsequently rejected by the following verifier window.

Real embedding, dense, attention/KV, PLE, GDN, router, expert, shared-expert, reduction and head kernels execute. At a graph boundary after the real router and before resident planning, both replay arms impose the same recorded IDs and float32 mixture coefficients. Each MTP head is computed before its recorded token/probability is imposed ahead of the next draft step. Main head results are computed before host-side replay overrides. Confidence stopping, acceptance, commit prefix, token feedback and output-budget stopping therefore reproduce the tape. Replay output equality is enforced, not evidence of mathematical correctness.

The binary format records prompt IDs, initial slot identities and native heat, window positions/shapes/inputs/outputs/accepted prefix, every main route/weight batch, all MTP routes/weights and draft tokens/probabilities. GPU route-invocation counts are checked after each window. Selected eight-component activations and native-head disagreements are observations and excluded from the work hash. Times, residency paths, admissions and evictions are also excluded. A SHA256 work fingerprint will accompany the binary's FNV integrity fingerprint.

The timed fixed-work scope is decode after normal warmup and prefill. Prefill is executed normally in every arm from the exact input IDs and same frontend. Its routes are not overridden in this prototype. Initial post-prefill expert sets, slot IDs and heat must match exactly. Further initial-state and shape audits precede any causal speed interpretation. Tape data are loaded in RAM before replay timing; oracle transfers are forbidden before the observed decode boundary.

Mode is OFF without explicit research environment variables. Recording is diagnostic and its overhead is excluded from speed claims. REPLAY_CURRENT and REPLAY_ORACLE share the same experimental executable, lookup machinery, recorded computation and overrides. Natural execution versus replay-current on development input quantifies replay overhead; a materially different critical path requires repair or scoped interpretation.

The current prototype assumes frozen serial spec=4, no suffix, no coupled sampling, two stages K=24, MTP on stage1, no split verifier groups. Unsupported shapes fail rather than silently approximate the tape. Normal serving launchers remain unchanged.
